<h3 id="scaling-considerations">Scaling Considerations</h3>
<p>The batch economics are favorable to scale because:</p>
<ul>
<li>Most fixed costs (equipment, setup) are absorbed in first year</li>
<li>Labor efficiency improves with experience and routine</li>
<li>Bulk purchasing reduces per-batch ingredient costs</li>
<li>Customer retention is high (monthly subscription product)</li>
</ul>
<p>Two operators working part-time (40 hours/month combined) can manage a four-tank operation with good systems.</p>
<h2 id="pricing-strategy">Pricing Strategy</h2>
<p><strong><code class="language-math math-inline">35/jar (midpoint of </code>32–38 range):</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Premium fermented food positioning (comparable to high-end kimchi/kombucha at $15–20/16oz)</li>
<li>The 6oz concentrated format delivers equivalent compound dose to a much larger conventional ferment</li>
<li>Monthly subscription ($35/month) is psychologically positioned as a food/supplement hybrid, not a pharmaceutical</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Price anchoring:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Quality supplements for the conditions targeted often run $100–300/month</li>
<li>The product at $35/month is dramatically less expensive than the supplement stack it partially replaces</li>
<li>At the same time, it's a premium food product with a compelling story</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="the-seasonal-botanicals-premium">The Seasonal Botanicals Premium</h2>
<p>Rotating botanical packs with seasonal and specialty ingredients (hawthorn berries at peak, locally harvested centella, etc.) creates a foundation for limited-release or premium-tier products:</p>
<ul>
<li>"Spring harvest batch" with fresh centella, fresh herbs</li>
<li>"Autumn botanical batch" with hawthorn, elder, rosehip additions</li>
<li>Premium pricing on these batches: $45–55 per jar</li>
</ul>
