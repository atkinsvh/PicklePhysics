Botanical Pack

Complete ingredient matrix for the 40-gallon medicinal ferment. 55–60kg total in nylon mesh bags organized for staggered monthly rotation.

Ingredient Weight % Primary compound(s) Primary target

Rotation Schedule

Stagger replacement weekly to maintain continuous compound concentration:

  • Week 1: Replace B1 bags
  • Week 2: Replace B2 bags
  • Week 3: Replace B3 bags
  • Week 4: Replace B4 bags

Each group replaced monthly. Never replace all groups simultaneously — this would temporarily drop compound concentrations. The staggered system maintains steady-state compound availability.

Special Handling Notes

Botanical Warning
Licorice root Max 5g/L brine — glycyrrhizin affects aldosterone at high doses
He shou wu (if used) Processed form only — raw is hepatotoxic
Horse chestnut Small amounts — dose-sensitive saponin
Vitex Not for use during pregnancy
Dan shen Monitor if on blood thinners — anticoagulant activity
