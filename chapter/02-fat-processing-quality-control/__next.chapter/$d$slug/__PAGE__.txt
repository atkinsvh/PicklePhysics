<h2 id="the-reference-sample-system">The Reference Sample System</h2>
<p>Keep a sealed small jar of freshly processed stearin as a permanent standard:</p>
<ul>
<li>Best available quality, freshly processed</li>
<li>Store dark, cool, sealed</li>
<li>Replace every 6 months</li>
<li>Use for: color comparison, odor comparison, water drop comparison</li>
<li>Photograph each test for trend tracking</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Reference standard specs:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>FFA: &#x3C;0.3%</li>
<li>Color: ivory-white</li>
<li>Odor (warm): neutral, mildly fatty</li>
<li>Water drop: discrete bead maintained</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="ffa-titration-full-procedure">FFA Titration — Full Procedure</h2>
<p><strong>Materials:</strong> IPA (99%), phenolphthalein indicator, 0.1M NaOH (4g food-grade NaOH per liter distilled water), scale (±0.1g), syringe or dropper</p>
<ol>
<li>Weigh 5.0g melted fat exactly</li>
<li>Add 10ml IPA, warm to 50°C until clear</li>
<li>Add 3 drops phenolphthalein (solution colorless)</li>
<li>Add 0.1M NaOH drop by drop, swirling between drops</li>
<li>Endpoint: first persistent pink lasting 30 seconds</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Calculation:</strong> FFA% = (ml NaOH × 0.1 × 282 × 100) ÷ (sample weight × 1000)</p>
<p><strong>Example:</strong> 0.5ml NaOH, 5.0g sample → FFA = (0.5 × 0.1 × 282 × 100) ÷ (5000) = 0.28%</p>
<p><strong>Acceptance criteria:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>&#x3C;0.5%: Release — meets specification</li>
<li>0.5–1.0%: Acceptable — monitor</li>
<li>
<blockquote>
<p>1.0%: Reprocess — additional ethanol wash + repeat Phase A</p>
</blockquote>
</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="sensory-tests">Sensory Tests</h2>
<p><strong>Paper towel test:</strong>
Dip white paper towel in fat at 60°C. Clean fat = even pale yellow absorption, no dark patches. Degraded fat = dark patches, uneven absorption, visible particulate residue. Run after each bentonite treatment — endpoint when successive treatments produce no improvement.</p>
<p><strong>Water drop test (FFA proxy):</strong>
Place fat drop on clean dry glass. Add water drop. Pass = discrete water bead maintains surface tension. Fail = water spreads and partially emulsifies (FFAs acting as emulsifiers at fat-water interface).</p>
<p><strong>Baking soda effervescence:</strong>
Add 5ml baking soda solution to 50ml fat sample. Vigorous sustained bubbling = high FFA. Mild brief = moderate. None = low FFA. Use as incoming quality assessment and post-processing check.</p>
<h2 id="bentonite-saturation-indicator">Bentonite Saturation Indicator</h2>
<p>Fresh bentonite: pale tan to grey-white. Spent bentonite: darkens toward amber-brown proportional to oxidized compound load.</p>
<ul>
<li>Spent bentonite exits already dark → fat had high contamination load, continue treatment</li>
<li>Spent bentonite similar to fresh → fat was relatively clean, treatment is complete</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Diminishing returns endpoint:</strong> Plot FFA or color measurement after each treatment. Continue until the improvement curve flattens — where another treatment produces no measurable improvement. This is both the quality endpoint and the economic optimization point.</p>
