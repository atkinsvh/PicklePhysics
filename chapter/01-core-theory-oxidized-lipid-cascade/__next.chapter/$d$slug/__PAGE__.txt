<blockquote>
<p><em>The complete causal chain from dietary oxidized fat to systemic disease. Every node is mechanistically characterized in peer-reviewed literature.</em></p>
</blockquote>
<h2 id="the-full-chain">The Full Chain</h2>
<pre><code>Dietary oxidized fat (OXLAMs, 4-HNE, peroxides)
        ↓
Gut epithelium: 4-HNE adducts on occludin, claudin-1, ZO-1
        ↓
Intestinal permeability increases
        ↓
LPS translocates into portal circulation (from dysbiotic gram-negative bacteria)
        ↓
Hepatic glutathione depleted processing OXLAMs → systemic distribution
        ↓
OXLAMs activate TLR4 on mast cells, macrophages, dendritic cells
        ↓
NF-κB → systemic inflammatory baseline elevated
        ↓
MERTK and AXL efferocytosis receptors inactivated by OxLDL/OXLAMs
        ↓
Efferocytosis fails → apoptotic cells undergo secondary necrosis
        ↓
Nuclear material (DNA, histones, citrullinated proteins) released
        ↓
TLR7/TLR9 activation on B cells → autoantibody production
        ↓
Estrogen dominance (beta-glucuronidase from dysbiotic gut) removes Treg brake
        ↓
B cell hyperreactivity to self-antigens → autoimmune cascade
        ↓
4-HNE adducts on prolyl hydroxylase + lysyl oxidase
        ↓
Collagen synthesis and cross-linking impaired → connective tissue failure
        ↓
4-HNE adducts on histone proteins H2A, H3, H4
        ↓
Aberrant CpG methylation at anti-inflammatory gene promoters
        ↓
Epigenetic inflammatory programming → transgenerational transmission
</code></pre>
<h2 id="key-molecules">Key Molecules</h2>
<h3 id="4-hydroxynonenal-4-hne">4-Hydroxynonenal (4-HNE)</h3>
<ul>
<li>Primary toxic aldehyde from linoleic acid oxidation</li>
<li>Bifunctional electrophile — forms covalent adducts with proteins, DNA, lipids</li>
<li>Specifically inhibits: prolyl hydroxylase, lysyl oxidase, Complex I/II (mitochondria), MERTK/AXL (efferocytosis), autophagy machinery</li>
<li>Genotoxic and cytotoxic at concentrations found in end-of-life fryer oil</li>
<li>Modifies histone proteins → epigenetic programming</li>
<li>→ See [[Lipid Oxidation Chemistry]]</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="oxlams-oxidized-linoleic-acid-metabolites">OXLAMs (Oxidized Linoleic Acid Metabolites)</h3>
<ul>
<li>9-HODE, 13-HODE, 9-oxoODE, 13-oxoODE</li>
<li>Direct TLR4 agonists — same receptor as LPS</li>
<li>Activate mast cells through CD36 and TLR4</li>
<li>Accumulate in adipose tissue as reservoir</li>
<li>→ See [[Lipid Oxidation Chemistry]]</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="total-polar-compounds-tpc">Total Polar Compounds (TPC)</h3>
<ul>
<li>The aggregate non-volatile toxic burden</li>
<li>Cannot be reduced by deodorization</li>
<li>The only fraud-resistant quality measure</li>
<li>European standard: 25–27% maximum</li>
<li>US: no standard exists</li>
<li>→ See [[TPC Standards]]</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="the-efferocytosis-node">The Efferocytosis Node</h2>
<p>This is the initiating event for autoimmune disease in this framework. See [[Efferocytosis Failure]] for full mechanism.</p>
<h2 id="the-estrogen-node">The Estrogen Node</h2>
<p>Beta-glucuronidase from dysbiotic gram-negative bacteria deconjugates estrogen glucuronides → estrogen recirculation → estrogen dominance → TLR7/TLR9 upregulation on B cells. See [[Estrobolome]].</p>
<h2 id="the-epigenetic-node">The Epigenetic Node</h2>
<p>4-HNE adducts on histones + MDA modification of CpG sites = persistent inflammatory epigenetic program. See [[Epigenetic Transmission]].</p>
