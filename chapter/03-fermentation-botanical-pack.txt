<h2 id="rotation-schedule">Rotation Schedule</h2>
<p>Stagger replacement weekly to maintain continuous compound concentration:</p>
<ul>
<li>Week 1: Replace B1 bags</li>
<li>Week 2: Replace B2 bags</li>
<li>Week 3: Replace B3 bags</li>
<li>Week 4: Replace B4 bags</li>
</ul>
<p>Each group replaced monthly. Never replace all groups simultaneously — this would temporarily drop compound concentrations. The staggered system maintains steady-state compound availability.</p>
