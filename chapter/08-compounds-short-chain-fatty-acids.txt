20:T1095,<h1 id="short-chain-fatty-acids">Short Chain Fatty Acids</h1>
<blockquote>
<p><em>The primary output of healthy fermentation. Not just metabolic byproducts — active epigenetic regulators and immune modulators.</em></p>
</blockquote>
<h2 id="production">Production</h2>
<p>SCFAs are produced by colonic fermentation of dietary fiber and resistant starch:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Butyrate (C4):</strong> predominantly from Faecalibacterium prausnitzii, Roseburia, Bifidobacterium on inulin-type fructans and resistant starch</li>
<li><strong>Propionate (C3):</strong> from Bacteroidetes on pectin and some hemicelluloses</li>
<li><strong>Acetate (C2):</strong> from most gut bacteria, abundant but less specific in function</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="butyrate-the-central-molecule">Butyrate — The Central Molecule</h2>
<h3 id="hdac-inhibition">HDAC Inhibition</h3>
<p>Butyrate is a competitive inhibitor of Class I and II histone deacetylases (zinc-dependent). At physiological concentrations produced by a healthy microbiome:</p>
<ul>
<li>Upregulates H3K27 acetylation at anti-inflammatory gene loci</li>
<li>Specifically activates FOXP3 promoter → Treg differentiation</li>
<li>Upregulates IL-10 promoter → anti-inflammatory cytokine</li>
<li>Upregulates Nrf2 target genes</li>
<li>Simultaneously drives tight junction protein gene expression</li>
</ul>
<p>This is the microbiome performing real-time epigenetic repair. The dysbiotic microbiome stopped doing this. Restoring Faecalibacterium abundance restores continuous epigenetic maintenance that was silently operating in healthy guts.</p>
<h3 id="colonocyte-fuel">Colonocyte Fuel</h3>
<p>Butyrate is the primary energy source for colonocytes (~70% of their energy). Colonocytes without butyrate are like cells without ATP. Tight junction maintenance is energetically expensive and is one of the first functions to fail under energy deficit.</p>
<h3 id="gpr109a-signaling">GPR109a Signaling</h3>
<p>Butyrate receptor on colonocytes and immune cells. GPR109a activation → IL-18 production → epithelial repair cascade independent of HDAC mechanism.</p>
<h3 id="mast-cell-stabilization">Mast Cell Stabilization</h3>
<p>Direct mast cell stabilization effect independent of both HDAC inhibition and GPR109a — through a mechanism involving reduced intracellular calcium signaling.</p>
<h2 id="supplementation-strategy">Supplementation Strategy</h2>
<p><strong>Sodium butyrate (600mg with each meal):</strong> Direct delivery during the gut microbiome re-establishment period. Provides the same signal the healthy microbiome would have been providing continuously.</p>
<p><strong>Tributyrin:</strong> Prodrug form — three butyrate molecules esterified to glycerol. Better stability, slower release, potentially more distal colon delivery. Alternative to sodium butyrate.</p>
<p><strong>PHGG (partially hydrolyzed guar gum):</strong> Prebiotic specifically feeding butyrate-producing Firmicutes species. Produces more colonic butyrate than most other prebiotics. Tasteless, completely soluble, can be added to anything.</p>
<p><strong>Long-chain inulin (from chicory root in formulation):</strong> Reaches the distal colon where the most vulnerable epithelium is. Short-chain fructooligosaccharides are fermented more proximally.</p>
<h2 id="propionate">Propionate</h2>
<ul>
<li>Weaker HDAC inhibitor than butyrate</li>
<li>FFAR2/FFAR3 receptor signaling</li>
<li>Hepatic gluconeogenesis substrate (relevant to metabolic syndrome)</li>
<li>Specific effect on adipose tissue biology</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="acetate">Acetate</h2>
<ul>
<li>Most abundant SCFA</li>
<li>Peripheral tissue fuel</li>
<li>FFAR2 receptor signaling on immune cells</li>
<li>Enters host one-carbon metabolism as acetyl-CoA</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="connections">Connections</h2>
<ul>
<li>[[Gut Microbiome Ecology]] — production context</li>
<li>[[Phase 1 - Gut Barrier]] — the primary gut intervention</li>
<li>[[One Carbon Metabolism]] — acetate → acetyl-CoA connection</li>
<li>[[NAD Pool]] — butyrate as HDAC inhibitor complements sirtuin HDAC on different loci</li>
<li>[[Mast Cell Biology]] — direct stabilization effect</li>
<li>[[System Architecture]] — fermentation producing these in the preparation</li>
</ul>
