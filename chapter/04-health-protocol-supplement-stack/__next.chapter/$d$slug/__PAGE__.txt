<li><strong>Intermittent fasting 16:8:</strong> mTOR-dependent autophagy</li>
<li><strong>Sauna (if accessible):</strong> HSP70/90; protein quality control</li>

<li><strong>Fresh broccoli sprouts:</strong> sulforaphane — can't substitute with supplement equivalently</li>
