3:T132c,<h1 id="tet-enzymes-active-dna-demethylation">TET Enzymes — Active DNA Demethylation</h1>
<blockquote>
<p><em>The only pathway for actively removing aberrant DNA methylation marks. Requires four simultaneous inputs — the absence of any one prevents the reaction.</em></p>
</blockquote>
<h2 id="what-tet-enzymes-do">What TET Enzymes Do</h2>
<p>TET1, TET2, TET3 are dioxygenases that oxidize 5-methylcytosine (5mC) to 5-hydroxymethylcytosine (5hmC):</p>
<pre><code>5mC + O₂ + α-ketoglutarate → 5hmC + CO₂ + succinate
</code></pre>
<p>5hmC is then further oxidized to 5-formylcytosine (5fC) and 5-carboxylcytosine (5caC), which are recognized by TDG (thymine-DNA glycosylase) and replaced with unmodified cytosine through base excision repair. This is true active demethylation — removal of the methyl group without DNA replication.</p>
<p><strong>Why this matters:</strong> DNMT-mediated methylation can be passively diluted by cell division (if maintenance methylation is impaired), but active removal requires TET enzyme function. Aberrant methylation at anti-inflammatory gene promoters written by 4-HNE/MDA persists until TET enzymes remove it.</p>
<h2 id="the-four-requirements">The Four Requirements</h2>
<h3 id="1-alpha-ketoglutarate-kg-primary-cosubstrate">1. Alpha-Ketoglutarate (α-KG) — Primary Cosubstrate</h3>
<p>Consumed in the reaction (converted to succinate). Must be continuously supplied from the TCA cycle.</p>
<p><strong>TCA cycle dependence:</strong> α-KG is produced from isocitrate by isocitrate dehydrogenase. This reaction requires functional mitochondria running the TCA cycle. 4-HNE adducts on isocitrate dehydrogenase impair this step. <strong>Mitochondrial restoration is a prerequisite for TET enzyme function — another reason Phase 0 must come first.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Direct supplementation:</strong> Calcium alpha-ketoglutarate can supply TET cosubstrate without requiring complete mitochondrial restoration first — a practical bridge.</p>
<h3 id="2-ferrous-iron-fe-metal-cofactor">2. Ferrous Iron (Fe²⁺) — Metal Cofactor</h3>
<p>Iron must be in the ferrous (Fe²⁺) state in the enzyme's active site. During the catalytic cycle, Fe²⁺ is oxidized to Fe³⁺ and must be reduced back to Fe²⁺ before the next reaction can occur. Without this reduction, TET becomes permanently oxidized and inactive.</p>
<h3 id="3-ascorbate-vitamin-c-the-iron-reducer">3. Ascorbate (Vitamin C) — The Iron Reducer</h3>
<p>Ascorbate specifically reduces Fe³⁺ back to Fe²⁺ in TET's active site, completing the catalytic cycle. Without ascorbate:</p>
<ul>
<li>TET runs 1–2 catalytic cycles</li>
<li>Iron oxidizes to Fe³⁺</li>
<li>TET becomes inactive (uncompetitively inhibited)</li>
<li>Active demethylation stops entirely</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>The functional deficiency problem:</strong> Under chronic oxidative stress, ascorbate is consumed as an antioxidant faster than diet supplies it. This produces functional vitamin C deficiency for TET enzyme purposes even with normal dietary intake. Liposomal vitamin C specifically addresses this by achieving higher plasma concentrations than gut-limited absorption allows.</p>
<h3 id="4-molecular-oxygen">4. Molecular Oxygen</h3>
<p>Required as the oxygen source in the dioxygenase reaction. Not a practical limiting factor in normal physiology but relevant under severe tissue hypoxia (relevant for understanding adipose remodeling where hypoxic adipose has impaired TET function).</p>
<h2 id="the-4-hne-deadlock">The 4-HNE Deadlock</h2>
<p>The same 4-HNE that writes aberrant methylation also disables the repair machinery:</p>
<ol>
<li>4-HNE writes inappropriate methylation at anti-inflammatory gene promoters (histone adducts altering chromatin accessibility)</li>
<li>4-HNE impairs Complex I/II → mitochondrial dysfunction → reduced TCA cycle flux → reduced α-KG</li>
<li>4-HNE impairs isocitrate dehydrogenase directly (documented adduct target)</li>
<li>Oxidative stress from mitochondrial dysfunction consumes ascorbate (reducing TET Fe²⁺ maintenance capacity)</li>
<li>Net result: TET enzymes have no cosubstrate (α-KG) and no iron reducer (ascorbate) → demethylation stalls</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Breaking the deadlock:</strong> Must address both α-KG supply (calcium α-KG supplement + mitochondrial restoration) AND ascorbate supply (liposomal vitamin C) simultaneously. Either alone is insufficient.</p>
