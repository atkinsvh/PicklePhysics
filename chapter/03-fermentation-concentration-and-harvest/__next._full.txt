20:T11e4,<h1 id="concentration-and-harvest">Concentration and Harvest</h1>
<blockquote>
<p><em>From 40-gallon ferment to 6oz concentrated jar delivering therapeutic dose in one teaspoon daily.</em></p>
</blockquote>
<h2 id="the-concentration-target">The Concentration Target</h2>
<p><strong>Current formulation:</strong> 1oz (28g) three times daily = 84g/day
<strong>Target format:</strong> 1 teaspoon (5ml) daily from 6oz jar = one jar per month</p>
<p><strong>Required concentration:</strong> 3–4× current formulation</p>
<p>Achieved through two compounding methods:</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>70/30 botanical:brine ratio</strong> (vs. standard 60:40) → 1.5× botanical density</li>
<li><strong>Freeze concentration of brine to 35% original volume</strong> → 2.8× brine concentration</li>
<li><strong>Combined effect:</strong> ~3–4× total</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="freeze-concentration-primary-method">Freeze Concentration (Primary Method)</h2>
<p>No heat, no live culture kill, no thermal degradation:</p>
<ol>
<li>Draw brine from mid-level of vessel (not top or bottom) — 25–30 liters per harvest cycle</li>
<li>Pour into shallow stainless trays</li>
<li>Freeze solid at −20°C (standard freezer)</li>
<li>Allow to begin thawing — drain the concentrated liquid as the first fraction thaws</li>
<li>Water (ice) crystallizes first, concentrating all dissolved compounds in the remaining liquid</li>
<li>Discard ice fraction (essentially pure water, compound-depleted)</li>
<li>Collect concentrated brine: 3–5× original compound concentration, live culture preserved</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Why the first fraction is most concentrated:</strong> Ice nucleates from pure water preferentially. The last liquid to freeze contains the most solutes. The first liquid to thaw (as temperature rises back above 0°C) is the most concentrated fraction.</p>
<h2 id="botanical-matter-harvest">Botanical Matter Harvest</h2>
<p>Open mesh bags. Extract minced botanical matter into collection vessel. This is NOT spent material:</p>
<p><strong>What remains in the botanical matter after 120-day aqueous extraction:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>All highly lipophilic compounds (AKBA logP 6–8, carnosic acid logP 4–5, thymol logP 3.3) — essentially no aqueous extraction</li>
<li>Intact fiber matrix with prebiotic function undiminished</li>
<li>Fermentation-enhanced compounds (biotransformed to more bioavailable forms)</li>
<li>Phytic acid reduced (mineral bioavailability improved)</li>
<li>Piperine concentrated in softened peppercorns (consume peppercorn = consume piperine)</li>
<li>Thymoquinone in softer, more accessible nigella seed matrix</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>The relish principle:</strong> Brine + botanical matter together are superior to either alone. They are complementary delivery mechanisms for different compound polarity classes.</p>
<h2 id="product-assembly">Product Assembly</h2>
<ol>
<li>Combine concentrated brine with botanical matter at 40% brine / 60% botanical ratio</li>
<li>Fill 6oz mason jars</li>
<li>Use one-way valve lids for live product (CO₂ exit without O₂ entry)</li>
<li>Quality check: pH 3.0–3.5 in concentrated brine, complex sour-herbal aroma, characteristic trigeminal profile present</li>
<li>Label: batch number, harvest date, pH, botanical pack version</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="yield-calculation-40-gallon-batch">Yield Calculation (40-gallon batch)</h2>
<ul>
<li>Brine volume: 91L → concentrated to ~25L (×3.5 concentration)</li>
<li>Botanical matter: ~55kg</li>
<li>Product per jar: ~177ml (6oz), 60% botanical / 40% brine</li>
<li><strong>Approximate yield: 200–225 jars per batch</strong></li>
<li>At staggered 4-tank operation: ~200 jars monthly</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="serving-protocol">Serving Protocol</h2>
<p><strong>Dose:</strong> 1 teaspoon (5ml) daily at 3–4× concentration
<strong>Timing:</strong> With a fat-containing meal — dietary fat solubilizes lipophilic compounds from botanical matter during digestion (the lecithin system in the brine assists, but meal-fat absorption through the gut mucosa is the primary lipophilic absorption route)
<strong>Introduction:</strong> Start ½ teaspoon daily. Increase gradually. Use DAO enzyme supplement for first 4–6 weeks if histamine-sensitive.</p>
