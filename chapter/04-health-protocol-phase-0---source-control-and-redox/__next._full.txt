20:T1e04,<h1 id="phase-0-source-control-and-redox-foundation">Phase 0 — Source Control and Redox Foundation</h1>
<blockquote>
<p><em>Non-negotiable. Every subsequent phase depends on this one. The autophagic machinery, collagen synthesis enzymes, TET demethylation system, and mast cell stabilization interventions all operate in a redox environment that must be functional first.</em></p>
</blockquote>
<h2 id="why-phase-0-must-come-first">Why Phase 0 Must Come First</h2>
<p>The core deadlock: [[4-HNE]] impairs the very machinery that would repair the damage it causes.</p>
<ul>
<li>4-HNE on Complex I/II → mitochondrial dysfunction → reduced alpha-ketoglutarate → [[TET Enzymes]] can't demethylate aberrant methylation</li>
<li>Oxidative DNA damage → PARP hyperactivation → NAD⁺ depletion → sirtuins can't deacetylate pro-inflammatory chromatin marks</li>
<li>Glutathione depletion → GPx non-functional → more lipid peroxidation → more 4-HNE</li>
</ul>
<p>Restoring the redox environment is not one intervention among many. It is the prerequisite that makes every other intervention possible.</p>
<h2 id="interventions-weeks-1-4">Interventions — Weeks 1–4</h2>
<h3 id="fat-source-control-immediate">Fat Source Control (Immediate)</h3>
<p><strong>Switch cooking fat to:</strong> beef tallow, lard, or clarified butter (ghee).</p>
<p>These are predominantly saturated and monounsaturated. They do not generate the linoleic acid oxidation cascade that produces 4-HNE and OXLAMs at frying temperatures. This single change eliminates the primary ongoing MERTK/AXL impairment signal, reduces hepatic glutathione demand, and stops the continuous TLR4 activation from dietary OXLAMs.</p>
<p>Implement [[11-Step Protocol]] for fat maintenance. Begin [[In-Fryer Antioxidants]] immediately.</p>
<p><strong>Eliminate:</strong> soybean oil, canola oil, corn oil, sunflower oil, vegetable oil, anything labeled vegetable shortening for regular cooking.</p>
<h3 id="nac-n-acetylcysteine-600mg-twice-daily">NAC (N-Acetylcysteine) — 600mg twice daily</h3>
<p>Provides cysteine, the rate-limiting precursor for glutathione synthesis. Glycine is the second independently limiting precursor (not cysteine alone — both must be present). NAC + glycine together restore glutathione more effectively than either alone.</p>
<p>Clinical use precedent: used in emergency medicine for acetaminophen overdose precisely because it rapidly restores hepatic glutathione.</p>
<h3 id="glycine-3-5g-daily">Glycine — 3–5g daily</h3>
<p>The second glutathione precursor. Also: one-third of collagen by residue, GlyR mast cell stabilizer, one-carbon metabolism substrate, NMDA co-agonist (sleep), bile acid conjugation. Tasteless. Dissolves in anything. One of the highest leverage-per-dollar interventions in the entire protocol. → [[Glycine]]</p>
<h3 id="liposomal-vitamin-c-1g-twice-daily">Liposomal Vitamin C — 1g twice daily</h3>
<p>Liposomal form achieves significantly higher plasma concentrations than standard oral ascorbic acid (gut absorption is limited by SVCT transporters).</p>
<p><strong>Functions in this context:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>[[TET Enzymes]] cofactor — maintains Fe²⁺ (ferrous state) required for TET oxidation of 5mC; without ascorbate, TET runs 1–2 cycles and stops</li>
<li>Prolyl and lysyl hydroxylase cofactor — collagen synthesis</li>
<li>Antioxidant network recycling — regenerates vitamin E</li>
<li>Functionally deficient under chronic oxidative stress even without dietary deficiency</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="sulforaphane-daily-from-broccoli-sprouts-or-supplement">Sulforaphane — Daily from Broccoli Sprouts or Supplement</h3>
<p><strong>Why sulforaphane is uniquely valuable:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Irreversible Keap1 modification</strong> → 24–48hr sustained Nrf2 activation</li>
<li>All other Nrf2 activators (curcumin, resveratrol, quercetin) form reversible bonds — activity drops when they're cleared</li>
<li>Sulforaphane's covalent modification lasts until Keap1 is replaced by new protein synthesis (~24–48h)</li>
<li><strong>Source specificity:</strong> Broccoli sprouts contain 50–100× higher sulforaphane than mature broccoli. 1–2 tablespoons fresh sprouts daily. The myrosinase enzyme required for sulforaphane formation is destroyed by cooking — sprouts must be consumed fresh or the supplement must contain stabilized active sulforaphane.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="astaxanthin-12mg-daily-with-fat-containing-meal">Astaxanthin — 12mg daily with fat-containing meal</h3>
<p>Unique structural properties: spans the full lipid bilayer (hydrophilic ends outside, hydrophobic middle), providing membrane protection that other antioxidants can't access. Specifically protective for mitochondrial membranes and renal tubular cells. → [[Terpenoids]]</p>
<h2 id="the-non-negotiable-sequence-logic">The Non-Negotiable Sequence Logic</h2>
<p>Starting mast cell stabilization or collagen synthesis supplementation before Phase 0 is established:</p>
<ul>
<li>Mast cell stabilizers (quercetin) work better in a lower oxidative environment</li>
<li>Collagen synthesis enzymes (prolyl hydroxylase, lysyl oxidase) are impaired by 4-HNE adducts — restoring them requires the redox environment that Phase 0 establishes</li>
<li>TET demethylation needs both ascorbate (Phase 0) and alpha-ketoglutarate (mitochondrial restoration) — neither works without the other</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Minimum Phase 0 establishment period: 2–4 weeks before adding Phase 2+ interventions.</strong></p>
<h2 id="concurrent-bile-axis-foundation">Concurrent Bile-Axis Foundation</h2>
<p>If upper GI dysbiosis is suspected or confirmed, the [[Bile-Micelle-Antioxidant Axis]] is also relevant in Phase 0:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Glycine 3–5g daily</strong> is non-optional — serves as glutathione precursor (this phase), mast cell GlyR stabilizer (Phase 2), and bile acid conjugation substrate (bile axis). <em>One supplement, three purposes.</em></li>
<li><strong>Continue low-dose wild vinegar (1 tbsp raw ACV in water, 15 min before largest meal)</strong> to begin cephalic phase restoration. See [[Wild Vinegars]].</li>
<li><strong>NAC at 1200mg/day (higher than the standard 600mg BID Phase 0 dose)</strong> also addresses upper GI biofilm matrix disruption. See [[Upper GI Biofilm Disruption]].</li>
<li>If H. pylori is suspected (chronic gastritis, halitosis, refractory iron deficiency), add <strong>mastic gum 1000–2000mg on empty stomach</strong> + <strong>lactoferrin 200–400mg</strong> to the Phase 0 stack.</li>
</ul>
<p>Phase 0's role in the bile axis is <em>not</em> the full intervention (that comes in the 5-layer [[Upper GI Biofilm Disruption]] protocol), but the foundation interventions overlap meaningfully — Phase 0 isn't replaced, it's <em>augmented</em>.</p>
