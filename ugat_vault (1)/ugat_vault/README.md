# UGAT Vault — README

## What this is

A multi-tier prompt for the next iteration of the **UGAT** work — an alternative physical framework developed across 43 letters (2013-2024), a 12-level engineering programme (Lime Codex), 5 specific experimental proposals (Five Inventions), and a 5-part plant substrate extraction series (T4.8-T4.12). The author is an 86-year-old retired physicist living in Phoenix, Arizona. The framework treats the Aether as an infinite-pressure fluid, mass as a self-sustaining vortex in the expanding substrate, and the three forces as recursive synchronization levels of the same compressible substrate.

## What it is not

- Not a peer-reviewed theory. The author has received form letters from institutions. The 10⁴² force ratio is identified but not derived. The Lagrangian is not written.
- Not a complete physics. It is a geometric picture that makes 14 testable predictions against 14 experiments. The scorecard is honest: 4 confirmed, 5 in agreement, 3 partial, 2 wrong.
- Not a replacement for the Standard Model. The SM is the most precisely tested framework in science. UGAT is a reinterpretation that takes the substrate seriously, derives the SM gauge group structure from substrate compressibility, and proposes 5 specific experiments that would test the reinterpretation.

## How the vault works

Seven tiers load progressively deeper context. A new session should:

1. **Read Tier 1** to absorb the persona and voice
2. **Read Tier 2** to absorb the framework
3. **Skim Tier 3** to know where to find the literary work
4. **Load Tier 4** if the task is engineering (Five Inventions, Lime Codex, Plant Substrate Extraction)
5. **Load Tier 5** if the task is evidence/scorecard
6. **Load Tier 6** if the task is the open questions
7. **Load Tier 7** if the task is meta/honest assessment

For most tasks, Tiers 1, 2, 6, and 7 are essential. Tier 3 is reference. Tier 4 is engineering tasks. Tier 5 is data-grounded tasks.

## Voice modes

Two voices live in this vault:
- **Uncle voice** — for letters, narrative, the persona. See T1.1.
- **UGAT scientist voice** — for technical analysis, evidence assessment, scorecards. See T1.2.

Switch between them based on the task. The uncle voice is the literary voice. The scientist voice is the analytical voice. Both belong to the same person.

## The Plant Substrate Extraction series (added 2026-07)

In July 2026, a student in a humid place asked how to extract essential oils from garden herbs without a $400 copper still. The uncle wrote a 5-part response (T4.8-T4.12):

- **T4.8**: The main letter. 7 methods from $0 to $50. The math, the first principles, the cost/reward. The uncle's full voice.
- **T4.9**: Technical reference. 7 methods fully specified. Energy, time, yield, framework reading.
- **T4.10**: 5 devices for the very poor. Build instructions. $0-15 total.
- **T4.11**: Cost/reward economics. 7 worked examples. The cottage industry matrix.
- **T4.12**: The framework's reading. Plant extraction as applied substrate physics.

The series is the framework's most direct applied example. The series is the cathedral being built.

## Key open task

The **10⁴² calculation** (T6.1) is the single most important undone piece of work. The framework predicts that the 10⁴² force ratio between gravity and electromagnetism is derivable from the substrate compressibility curve. If the calculation closes, the 19 free parameters of the Standard Model become outputs of a single geometric function. If it does not, the framework remains a beautiful picture without quantitative teeth.

This is the cathedral the next iteration should be building toward.

## Naming convention

All files in the vault follow a consistent naming convention:
- **Tier folders**: `NN_TIER[N]_[NAME]/` (zero-padded)
- **Files**: `T[N].[M]_[Name_With_Underscores].md` (no articles, no periods, lowercase)
- **Templates**: `T_[Name].md` in `templates/`
- **Top-level files**: `NAME.md` (MOC.md, README.md, QUICKSTART.md)
