# T — New Critique Template

Use this template when writing a critique of the framework — from the visitor, from a peer reviewer, or from the framework's own self-correction.

## Structure

```markdown
# Critique [N]: [Title]

## The target
[What part of the framework is being critiqued? Letter number, file reference, prediction number, etc.]

## The criticism
[What is wrong? What doesn't fit? What is missing?]

## The evidence
[What data or argument supports the criticism?]

## The alternative reading
[How would the standard physics interpret the same evidence?]

## The visitor's response
[What does the visitor say to the criticism? Does the visitor accept, modify, or reject?]

## The framework's response
[What does the framework say to the criticism? Does the framework accept, modify, or reject?]

## The status
[Is the criticism addressed? Is the framework updated? Is the criticism still open?]

## The honest summary
[What did this critique teach the framework? What did it leave open?]
```

## Example: Critique 1 — The Log Strong Force

## The target
- Framework prediction: residual strong force at nuclear boundary falls off logarithmically (from the substrate compressibility curve)
- Reference: Letter XXII, framework's general mechanism for the residual strong force

## The criticism
- **What is wrong**: The data says the residual strong force falls off exponentially (Yukawa form), not logarithmically
- **Evidence**: Multiple precision nuclear scattering experiments, including the Nijmegen partial wave analysis
- **Reference**: Stoks et al. PRC 48, 792 (1993); subsequent updates

## The alternative reading
- **Standard physics**: The residual strong force is mediated by σ, ω, ρ meson exchange, giving Yukawa form with meson-mass-determined ranges
- **Framework equivalent**: The Yukawa form is the substrate's response to the meson-arena geometry. The exponential falloff reflects the arena's natural length scale (set by the meson mass).

## The visitor's response
- The visitor (topological physicist) said: "your prediction is wrong"
- The visitor accepted the data as definitive
- The visitor noted the framework can accommodate the Yukawa form by adjusting the compressibility curve at the nuclear scale

## The framework's response
- The framework accepts the correction (in spirit — the original letters predicted log, the data says exp)
- The framework accommodates the Yukawa form as a correction to the compressibility curve
- The framework notes this is one of two "wrong" predictions in the 14-experiment scorecard

## The status
- **Status**: WRONG at >5σ
- **Framework updated**: Yes — the framework now predicts exponential Yukawa, not logarithmic
- **Critique still open**: No — the correction has been made

## The honest summary
- The framework got this prediction wrong
- The data is the data
- The framework accommodates the correction
- The scorecard is honest about the failure
- The cathedral is honest about its errors

This critique is one of two wrong predictions in the 14-experiment scorecard (T5.1). The other is the original prediction that the Kibble balances should converge — the framework now predicts they should disagree (lumpy well).

## New critique checklist

Before writing a new critique, check:

- [ ] Is the target specific? (Letter, file, prediction, mechanism)
- [ ] Is the criticism clear? (What is wrong, not just what is missing)
- [ ] Is the evidence cited? (Data, papers, calculations)
- [ ] Is the alternative reading given? (How standard physics interprets)
- [ ] Is the visitor's response honest? (Accept, modify, reject)
- [ ] Is the framework's response honest? (Accept, modify, reject)
- [ ] Is the status clear? (Addressed, open, partial)
- [ ] Is the honest summary given? (What did this teach)

If 7 of 8 are yes, write the critique. If fewer, revise.
