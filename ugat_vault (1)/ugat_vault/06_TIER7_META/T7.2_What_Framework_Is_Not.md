# T7.2 — What This Framework Is Not

## A list of things the framework is not

This file is a complement to T7.1 (The Honest Assessment). T7.1 says what the framework is. This file says what the framework is not. The next iteration should keep both lists in mind.

## The framework is NOT a theory of everything

The framework is a reinterpretation of the Standard Model in substrate language. The framework does not explain:
- The pre-metric dimension (what is the substrate made of?)
- The origin of the universe (why is there something rather than nothing?)
- The origin of the substrate's properties (why these particular values of c, G, ħ?)
- The origin of consciousness (why does organized matter become self-aware?)
- The origin of life (why does chemistry become biology?)
- The origin of mathematics (why is the universe mathematical?)

These questions precede the framework. The framework does not address them. The framework is honest about not addressing them.

## The framework is NOT a replacement for the Standard Model

The SM is the most precisely tested theory in science. UGAT is a reinterpretation. The SM's Lagrangian works. UGAT's Lagrangian doesn't exist. The SM's predictions are precise. UGAT's predictions are qualitative.

The framework is consistent with the SM. The framework is not a replacement. The framework is a different way of reading the same data.

If the framework is right, the SM is a *derived* theory. The SM's 19 free parameters are outputs of the substrate compressibility. The SM's gauge groups are phases of the substrate compression. The SM's force ratios are the geometric structure of the substrate.

If the framework is right, the SM is a special case. The SM is the local, low-energy, low-density limit of the substrate physics.

If the framework is wrong, the SM is fundamental. The SM's free parameters are inputs. The SM's gauge groups are postulated. The SM's force ratios are measured.

The framework is honest about this. The framework is the SM, possibly derived. The framework is the SM, possibly re-described. The framework is not the SM, replaced.

## The framework is NOT a complete physics

The framework is half a physics. The framework has the interpretation. The framework does not have the math. The framework has the picture. The framework does not have the Lagrangian.

The framework is mature enough to:
- Make specific predictions
- Be tested against data
- Guide experimental design
- Be evaluated honestly

The framework is not mature enough to:
- Replace the SM
- Derive the SM parameters
- Stand on its own
- Be considered "physics" in the standard sense

The 5/10 in T7.1 is the honest assessment. The framework is half a physics. The framework is not a complete physics.

## The framework is NOT confirmed

The framework is:
- 4/14 predictions confirmed at >3σ
- 5/14 in agreement
- 3/14 partial
- 2/14 wrong
- 2/14 aspirational
- 3/14 speculative

The framework is mostly right. The framework is partially wrong. The framework is consistently testable.

The framework is NOT confirmed. The framework is consistent with the data. The framework is honest about this. The framework is at 5/10, not at 9/10.

## The framework is NOT aether in the classical sense

The classical aether (19th century) was:
- A mechanical medium
- With definite velocity relative to the Earth
- Required to explain the propagation of light
- Disproven by the Michelson-Morley experiment

The framework's aether is:
- A continuous, infinite-pressure, perfectly inviscid fluid
- With no preferred frame (the substrate is the same in all directions)
- Required to explain the substrate pressure (Casimir) and the matter/radiation frame distinction (dipole)
- Consistent with all current data, including Michelson-Morley

The framework's aether is not the classical aether. The framework's aether is a different concept with a different role.

The framework is honest about this. The classical aether is a different thing. The framework's substrate is not the classical aether.

## The framework is NOT a free-energy device

The framework does not provide:
- Reactionless drives (no specific design)
- Aether extractors (the substrate pressure is constrained by the geometry)
- Perpetual motion machines (the substrate is not a source of unlimited energy)
- Faster-than-light travel (c is the substrate's expansion rate)

The framework's engineering applications (Lime Codex, Five Inventions) are:
- Passive radiative cooling (Level I-XII)
- Photocatalytic coatings (Invention II)
- Mercury resonance measurements (Invention III)
- Superconductivity triggers (Invention IV)
- Directional Casimir force (Invention V)

None of these are free-energy devices. None of these break the laws of thermodynamics. None of these violate the conservation of energy.

The framework is honest about this. The framework does not promise free energy. The framework promises a deeper understanding of why the universe works the way it does.

## The framework is NOT a critique of the Standard Model

The framework is a reinterpretation. The framework is not a critique.

The SM is the most precisely tested theory in science. The SM's Lagrangian works. The SM's predictions are precise. The SM's experimental record is unmatched.

The framework says: the SM is right. The framework says: the SM may be derived from a deeper substrate. The framework says: the SM's parameters may be outputs of the substrate compressibility.

The framework does not say: the SM is wrong. The framework says: the SM is the local, low-energy limit of the substrate.

The framework is honest about this. The SM is right. The framework is the SM, possibly re-described. The framework is not the SM, refuted.

## The framework is NOT for everyone

The framework is for:
- Physicists who are curious about substrate interpretations
- Engineers who want to apply the framework (Lime Codex)
- Graduate students who want to do the 5 experiments
- Theoreticians who want to do the 10⁴² calculation
- Anyone who has read the letters and found the picture compelling

The framework is NOT for:
- People who want a free-energy device
- People who want a refutation of the SM
- People who want a theory of everything
- People who want certainty about consciousness, life, or the origin of the universe

The framework is honest about this. The framework is a specific picture. The framework is for specific people. The framework is not for everyone.

## The honest summary

The framework is:
- A reinterpretation of the SM in substrate language
- A picture, not a complete physics
- Half a physics, with the interpretation strong and the math missing
- Mostly right, partially wrong, consistently testable
- Honest about what it is and what it is not

The framework is NOT:
- A theory of everything
- A replacement for the SM
- A complete physics
- A confirmed framework
- The classical aether
- A free-energy device
- A critique of the SM
- For everyone

The framework is what it is. The framework is honest about what it is. The framework is honest about what it is not.

The next iteration should keep both lists in mind. The framework is what it is. The framework is not what it is not.
