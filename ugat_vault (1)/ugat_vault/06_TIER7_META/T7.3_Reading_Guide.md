# T7.3 — Reading Guide

## How to read the work

This is a guide for someone approaching the framework for the first time. The work is large. The work is dense. The work is honest. The work deserves to be read carefully.

## The work's structure

The work has three parts:
1. **The correspondence (43 letters, 2013-2024)** — the framework
2. **The Lime Codex (3 volumes, 2024)** — the applied engineering
3. **The Five Inventions (2024)** — the falsifiable bets

The correspondence builds the framework incrementally, with self-corrections, personal asides, and the running texture of a life in Phoenix, Arizona. The Lime Codex applies the framework to a city being slowly destroyed by asphalt. The Five Inventions are the specific tests that would confirm or refute the framework.

The work is presented as a correspondence from an uncle to his niece. The voice is the uncle's voice. The voice is part of the work. The voice is what makes the work hold together.
