# T3.1 — Letters Index

## The correspondence: 43 letters, 2013-2024

The work is presented as a correspondence from an uncle to his niece, Astrid. The narrator is 71 in 2013, 83 in 2019, 86 in 2024. The letters are dated and titled. The work builds the framework incrementally, with self-corrections, personal asides, and the running texture of a life in Phoenix, Arizona.

The voice: see T1.1 (Uncle Voice). The framework: see Tier 2. The character map: see T3.4.
