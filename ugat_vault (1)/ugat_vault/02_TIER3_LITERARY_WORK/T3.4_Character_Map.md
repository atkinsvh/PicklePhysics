# T3.4 — Character Map

The work is a correspondence. The correspondence has characters. The characters are real (to the work) and limited (to the letters). The voice is shaped by the characters. The framework is shaped by the characters.

## The Uncle (the narrator)

- 71 in 2013, 83 in 2018, 86 in 2024
- Retired physicist (formal mathematics abandoned in 1963)
- Lives in Phoenix, Arizona
- The study is the primary location (chair, fern, parking lot visible)
- Writes the letters to his niece Astrid
- The framework is his life work
- The framework is the cathedral
- He is 83. He does not stop.

## Astrid (the niece)

- 22 in 2013, 32 in 2019
- The recipient of the letters
- The heir to the framework
- The one who will do the math
- The one who will run the experiments
- The one who will close the 10⁴² calculation
- She is a graduate student in physics
- She is the cathedral's next architect

## Bertrand (the cat)

- Died 2018, age 19
- "85.5 kilogram-years" of cost of existence
- The embodied proof of the framework
- Yawned at the uncle when he sat down without a plan
- Preferred the grey blanket (not the blue)
- Understood gravity intuitively (freefall is following the substrate)
- "Did the right thing without requiring a theory of the right thing"
- "Bertrand understood this intuitively. He never thought about it. He simply did it correctly every time."

## Margaret (the wife)

- Died 2018
- "Would have wanted the fern watered"
- The moral anchor of the work
- The reason the lime coat is for Phoenix
- The reason the framework is for people
- Margaret is not a character in the letters as a presence
- Margaret is a character in the letters as an absence
- The fern is watered for Margaret

## Gerald (the neighbor)

- Lives nearby
- Has not limed his driveway
- Has not read the letters
- "Gerald's capacity for reasonable conversation, which I overestimated in 2019 and have since recalibrated"
- The institutional resistance personified
- The form letter in human form
- Gerald is not malicious, just inert
- Gerald continues to make the neighborhood hotter
- The framework has done its part. Gerald has not done his.

## The Fern

- On the second shelf
- Has been there longer than the framework
- Needs water and indirect light
- Does not know it is a vortex complex
- "It does not know it is a vortex complex in the expansion front. It needs water and indirect light. These are the same thing."
- The smallest working model of the framework
- The proof that the substrate is also a plant

## The Porch Light

- Turned off on Halloween 2019 (Letter XLI)
- The children are ringing the bell
- The uncle is writing the last physics letter
- The porch light is the small social contract
- The framework is the larger one

## The Parking Lot

- 162°F
- Asphalt
- The practical reason for the framework
- The reason the lime exists
- "Phoenix, this week, 109°F at seven in the morning. The parking lot is 162°F. The atmospheric window is still open. The lime is still on the walls. We are doing what we can."

## Phoenix, Arizona

- The city
- 109°F at seven in the morning, 113°F by afternoon
- The framework's most direct application
- The Lime Codex is for Phoenix
- "The framework is not just a physics. The framework is a way of being in a city."
- "The way of being is: pay attention to the substrate. The substrate is the air, the water, the light, the heat, the surfaces, the people. Pay attention. Use what the framework gives you. The lime coat is the framework made into a wall."

## The Visitor (the topological physicist)

- Fictional / a thought experiment
- A projection of the framework
- "The kind of physicist I would have been if I had read the catalogues and not given up on the equation of state in 1963"
- Added: the data, the sieve, the lumpy well, the scorecard
- Did not add: the framework, the picture, the lime, the math problem
- In the conversation
- Honest
- The physicist the uncle did not become

## The Substrate (the Aether)

- Infinite-pressure fluid
- Continuous, perfectly inviscid
- Unfolding outward from the pre-metric dimension
- "We are the expansion, in its current state of organizational complexity at our location in the front."
- The substrate is the cathedral's stone
- The substrate is the cathedral's architect
- The substrate is the cathedral

## The Casimir Plates

- "Right now, in physics laboratories around the world, two metal plates separated by nanometers are being pushed together by the expansion pressure of the pre-metric substrate attempting to nucleate virtual particle pairs in the space between them."
- The direct measurement of the substrate
- The experimental anchor of the framework
- The proof that the substrate is real
- The proof that the framework is physics

## The Cathedral

- Bigger than the study
- Needs the equation of state
- Needs the math
- Needs the next move
- "The math is the cathedral. The cathedral needs the niece. The cathedral needs you."
