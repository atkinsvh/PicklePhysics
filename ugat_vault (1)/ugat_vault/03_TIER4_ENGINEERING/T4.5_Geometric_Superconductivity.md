# T4.5 — Invention IV: Geometric Superconductivity Trigger

## What it is

Continuous-wave phonon-mode excitation of YBCO above Tc — providing the pre-formation template rather than reducing thermal fluctuations. The first sustained zero-resistance state above Tc in a cuprate, if the framework is right.

## The framework basis

Transient superconductivity in YBa₂Cu₃O₆.₅ (YBCO) above Tc under intense mid-infrared pulses was reported by Fausti et al. (Science 2011) and confirmed in multiple subsequent studies. The Fausti experiment used 800 nm pump + OPA to generate 15 µm pulses exciting the Cu-O apical oxygen stretching mode at ~16 µm (680 cm⁻¹). The superconducting state appeared within picoseconds and decayed in ~1 ps — consistent with the coherence template being provided impulsively and the Cooper pair state forming before thermal equilibration destroys it.

The framework prediction: the mediating phonon mode for Cooper pair formation in YBCO is the B₁g buckling mode at 340 cm⁻¹ (29.4 µm, 10.2 THz) — the mode that modulates the Cu-O-Cu bond angle and creates the charge density wave that Cooper pairs condense into. Previous experiments excited the oxygen stretching mode (geometric displacement of apical oxygen) rather than this mediating mode directly.

```
Target phonon frequency: 340 cm⁻¹ = 10.2 THz = 29.4 µm wavelength
Corresponding photon energy: 42.2 meV
```

A continuous-wave THz source at 10.2 THz, applied to YBCO above Tc, should maintain the Cooper pair pre-formation continuously rather than transiently. The steady-state prediction: zero DC resistance above Tc as long as the THz drive is active.

## Apparatus requirements

- **THz source**: Continuous-wave at 10.2 THz (29.4 µm). Options: (a) Quantum cascade laser at 10 THz — Alpes Lasers, Daylight Solutions. Output 1-10 mW cw. $15,000-40,000. (b) Difference frequency generation from two near-IR lasers. (c) FTIR-based broadband + bandpass filter.
- **YBCO sample**: Thin film, 100-300 nm thick on STO substrate, patterned into bridge geometry for 4-probe resistance measurement. Tc (zero resistance): 88-93 K depending on oxygen stoichiometry. Required: well-characterised, stable over weeks. $500-2,000 for several samples.
- **Cryostat**: Variable temperature 77-150 K. Optical access for THz beam. Electrical feedthroughs for 4-probe resistance. Standard liquid nitrogen cryostat with optical window adequate.
- **Resistance measurement**: Lock-in amplifier at 1 kHz AC current, 1-10 µA amplitude. Resolution: 1 mΩ. Target resistance at superconducting state: <1 mΩ. Normal state: ~10-100 Ω. Contrast: 10⁴-10⁵.
- **THz power at sample**: 0.1-10 mW/cm². Damage threshold for YBCO thin film: >100 mW/cm² — wide safety margin.

## Experimental protocol

1. Characterise YBCO sample: measure R(T) from 300 K to 77 K. Record Tc (onset), Tc (zero resistance), residual resistance ratio.
2. Mount in cryostat with THz optical access. Align THz beam to sample active area.
3. Cool to Tc + 10 K (approximately 98-103 K for typical YBCO film).
4. Baseline resistance at this temperature with THz OFF: should be normal state value (~10-100 Ω). Record 10 min average.
5. THz ON at minimum power (0.1 mW/cm²). Record resistance vs. time for 10 min. Then increase power in steps: 0.3, 1, 3, 10 mW/cm². At each power: 10 min average resistance.
6. If resistance drops: verify it is not laser heating (heating would increase resistance, not decrease; if resistance decreases despite slight warming, it is the geometric trigger effect).
7. THz OFF: record resistance recovery. If the triggered superconducting state persists after the drive is switched off (even for milliseconds beyond the drive pulse), the pre-formation step has been completed and the state has locked in. This is the key result.
8. Frequency sweep: vary THz frequency ±20% around 10.2 THz. The effect should be frequency-specific — a resonance in the THz frequency dependence of the resistance drop, centered on the mediating phonon mode. This distinguishes the geometric mechanism from simple heating.

## Predicted results

- **Primary**: Resistance drop of >10× when THz drive at 10.2 THz is applied to YBCO at T = Tc + 5-15 K
- **Secondary**: Resistance drop is frequency-specific — peaks at B₁g phonon mode frequency
- **Tertiary**: At optimal drive frequency and power, complete zero resistance (within measurement resolution) persists as long as THz drive is active
- **Negative result**: If no resistance drop at 10.2 THz but drop at 16 µm (Fausti): the mediation pathway is correctly identified in prior work, the B₁g mode is not the trigger. Valuable information — narrows mechanism.
