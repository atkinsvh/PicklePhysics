## Apparatus specification

- **Container**: Quartz cylinder, 50 mm ID, 80 mm OD, 100 mm length. Sealed ends with quartz windows. Two platinum wire electrodes (0.5 mm) sealed through walls 180° apart at mid-height for Hall voltage measurement.
- **Mercury**: 99.9995% purity, 200 mL. ¹⁹⁹Hg isotopically enriched preferred (nuclear spin I=½, simpler NMR signal) but natural abundance acceptable for initial experiment. Enriched: ~$500/g. Natural: ~$80/200 mL.
- **Rotation stage**: Air bearing rotation stage, 0-5000 RPM, angular velocity stability ±0.1 RPM. Servo-controlled. Balanced to <0.1 g·cm residual imbalance. Aerotech ART300 or equivalent. ~$8,000 new, available used.
- **Magnetic field**: Superconducting solenoid 0-5 Tesla, bore >100 mm. OR: NdFeB permanent magnet array producing 0.5-1 Tesla uniform field over 100 mm bore. Significantly cheaper ($2,000-5,000 for custom assembly).
- **Signal measurement**: Lock-in amplifier (Stanford Research SR830 or equivalent), reference from rotation stage encoder. Time constant: 10 s. Full-scale sensitivity: 100 nV. Frequency range: 1 Hz to 10 kHz.
- **Slip rings**: Mercury-wetted slip rings for electrode signal extraction from rotating assembly. Moog or Mercotac.
- **Ventilation**: Sealed glove box or fume hood with mercury vapour monitor. NIOSH ceiling limit: 0.025 mg/m³.

## Experimental protocol

1. Load mercury container in ventilated enclosure. Seal fill port. Verify no leakage by weighing (mercury density 13.6 g/mL).
2. Mount container on rotation stage. Balance dynamically to <0.1 g·cm. Mount electrode leads to slip rings.
3. Position in magnetic field. Verify field uniformity across container volume to <0.1%.
4. Baseline measurement: rotate at 100 RPM (well below any resonance). Record Hall voltage vs. field strength from 0 to 2 Tesla. V_MHD ∝ B × Ω × r².
5. Calculate resonance rotation rates: Ω_res = ω_Larmor(B)/n for n = 1, 2, 3, 4... At B = 1 T for ¹⁹⁹Hg: Ω_res at n = 30,000: ~15 RPM; n = 3,000: ~154 RPM; n = 300: ~1542 RPM.
6. Slow sweep: ramp rotation from 50 to 5000 RPM at 1 RPM/minute. Record Hall voltage vs. rotation rate. Anomalous signal appears as sharp peak (Q factor ~10-100, width ~1-10 RPM) superimposed on smoothly varying standard MHD background.
7. At each candidate peak: hold rotation rate, vary magnetic field ±10%. Anomalous signal scales as (Zα)² — independent of field magnitude. Standard MHD signal scales as B. This field-dependence test distinguishes the two contributions.
8. Repeat with ²⁰¹Hg-enriched sample if available. Resonance rates shift by ratio of nuclear g-factors (0.5602/0.3887 = 1.441).
