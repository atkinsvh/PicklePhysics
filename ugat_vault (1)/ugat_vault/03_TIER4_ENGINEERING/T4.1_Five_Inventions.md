## The five falsifiable bets

The Five Inventions are the framework's *concrete, specific, falsifiable bets* on the substrate physics. Each has a defined apparatus, a defined procedure, a defined predicted signal, and a defined success criterion. Each is the framework's most direct experimental test.

The Five Inventions are presented in the *Five Inventions from the Vortex Framework* document (2024), in the same voice as the letters. The visitor organized them in a 5-year research program in 2026. The priority order is the visitor's contribution.

## Priority order (visitor's 5-year program)

The visitor's order was driven by: (a) decisiveness of the test, (b) cost, (c) time to result, (d) publishability. The order is:

### Year 1: Warm-up
- **Invention II: Au-Ag Bimetallic Photocatalytic Sensitizer** (T4.3) — Warm-up. Under $1,000. 1 day synthesis. Publishable per dollar. Lowest stakes scientifically.

### Year 1-2: The decisive test
- **Invention III: Mercury Spinning Experiment** (T4.4) — The decisive single experiment. $15,000. 5 months. Direct test of relativistic nuclear-electronic coupling. Most publishable in a top journal. The framework's most specific prediction.

### Year 2-3: The substrate test
- **Invention V: Casimir Geometry Engine** (T4.6) — Test of whether the substrate pressure can be made directional. $5,000-$15,000. 1 year. Theoretically most interesting.

### Year 3-4: The condensed matter test
- **Invention IV: Geometric Superconductivity Trigger** (T4.5) — Test of pre-formation principle applied to YBCO. $20,000-$80,000. 6 months. Requires collaboration with THz spectroscopy group.

### Year 4-5: The applied engineering test
- **Invention I: Topology-Optimised Lime Seal Coat** (T4.2) — Engineering test of coherent Seebeck chain. $500 in materials. 1 week for initial test. Practical, applied, city-scale.
