# T4.2 — Invention I: Topology-Optimised Lime Seal Coat

## What it is

A modified version of the Lime Codex Vol III seal coat with three topology additions that close the synchronization chain from photon interface to ground plane.

## The framework basis

The current Lime Codex Version 3 seal coat contains seven functional components (TiO₂, ZnO, CuO, NiO, CeO₂, fumed SiO₂, GO) producing four independent photocatalytic mechanisms. The synchronization chain analysis identifies four topology gaps:

1. **Photon interface / cascade boundary**: no 2D bridge material — TiO₂ and GO are both 3D particulate
2. **Cascade carrier transport**: GO provides electron highway but diffusion length ~100 nm
3. **Transduction / ground plane boundary**: no material bridges the C-S-H seal coat and the carbon black primer
4. **Ground plane anchor**: carbon black primer at ~10⁴ Ω/sq — adequate but not optimised

Prediction: closing all four gaps produces a Seebeck voltage increase greater than the sum of individual component contributions, because the coherent chain generates collective thermoelectric behavior not present in disconnected mechanisms.

## The three additions

### Addition 1: MoS₂ Nanosheets (2% by weight)
- 2D topology bridge at photon interface / cascade boundary
- Single or few-layer, lateral dimension 100-500 nm, thickness 1-3 nm
- Lattice match: MoS₂ (0001) plane 3.16 Å vs TiO₂ anatase (001) 3.78 Å (16% mismatch, acceptable)
- ~$40/gram, $4/100 sqft

### Addition 2: Lead-Free Perovskite (3% by weight)
- High carrier diffusion length cascade transport layer
- CsSnI₃ or MASnI₃ nanoparticles, 10-30 nm, passivated with oleylamine
- Carrier diffusion length: 100-200 µm vs GO's ~100 nm
- ~$6/100 sqft in precursors
- Sn-based oxidizes in air — Bi-based Cs₃Bi₂I₉ as air-stable fallback

### Addition 3: Bi₂Te₃ Nanoparticles in Carbon Black Primer (15% of CB weight)
- Relativistic topology bridge at transduction layer / ground plane boundary
- Bi₂Te₃ has Zα(Bi) ≈ 0.60, large-footprint vortex geometry
- Best known room-temperature thermoelectric (Seebeck ~200-300 µV/K)
- ~$25/10g, $1/100 sqft

## Modified application protocol

1. Prepare carbon black primer with Bi₂Te₃ addition. Apply. Allow 24 hr cure.
2. Prepare MoS₂ dispersion in isopropanol. Sonicate 60 min.
3. Synthesise perovskite nanoparticles or obtain commercial equivalent. Redisperse in hexane.
4. Prepare Version 3 dry blend adding MoS₂ sheets at 2% to the fumed SiO₂.
5. Prepare Version 3 seal coat solution. Add perovskite nanoparticles as hexane dispersion. Add GO last. Apply immediately.
6. Apply seal coat by ultrasonic spray nozzle. Sonophoresis during gelation.
7. After 96 hr cure: measure Seebeck voltage at solar noon. Compare to baseline.

## Predicted measurements

- **Seebeck voltage**: 40-80% above Version 3 baseline
- **Photocatalytic rate**: 2-3× improvement in standardised contamination test
- **FTIR emissivity**: Above 96% at 9.7 and 12.5 µm
- **Thermal performance**: Surface temp 3-8°F lower than Version 3

## Cost

- MoS₂: ~$4/100 sqft
- Perovskite: ~$6/100 sqft
- Bi₂Te₃: ~$1/100 sqft
- Total addition over Version 3: ~$11/100 sqft
- Total system with additions: ~$51/100 sqft at Level V
- Development cost: ~$500 for 100 sqft test section

## Failure modes

- **Perovskite oxidation**: Mitigation = Bi-based lead-free alternative
- **MoS₂ restacking**: Test with UV-vis (restacked shows different absorption onset). Mitigation = prepare immediately before use
- **Seebeck voltage not improved**: Diagnostic = measure with each addition separately. The addition whose contribution is lower than expected identifies the gap location.
