# T4.7 — Lime Codex Levels I-XII

## The 12-level applied engineering programme

The Lime Codex is the framework made practical. From a single lime wash on a balcony wall (Level I) to a city-scale reflective cooling system (Level XII). The substrate physics is the foundation. The 12 levels are the engineering pipeline.

The 12 levels are organized in three volumes:
- **Vol I: Levels I-VI** (the personal scale) — single wall, whole house, driveway, roof, walkway, garden
- **Vol II: Levels VII-IX** (the block scale) — block, photocatalytic stack, commons
- **Vol III: Levels X-XII** (the city scale) — street, district, city

The cost scales from $0.40/sqft (Level I) to ~$10B (Level XII). The cooling scales from 5-8°F (Level I) to 3-5°F (Level XII). The framework connection is direct: each level is the substrate's Y-axis synchronization at that scale.

## Volume I: Levels I-VI (the personal scale)

### Level I: The Single Wall
- **Application**: Lime wash on a single wall
- **Materials**: 1 part hydrated lime (calcium hydroxide, Ca(OH)₂) to 3 parts water. Slaked 24 hours. Applied by brush, roller, or spray
- **Cost**: $0.40/sqft
- **Cooling**: 5-8°F below ambient (single wall, no thermal mass)
- **Reflectance**: 75-85% solar
- **Emissivity**: 0.91 in 8-13 µm atmospheric window
- **Framework connection**: Photonic substrate response (Level I is the substrate's individual photon response)

### Level II: The Whole House
- **Application**: Lime wash on all exterior walls (south, west, east exposures prioritized)
- **Materials**: Same as Level I, 2,000 sqft average residential
- **Cost**: ~$500 for 2,000 sqft
- **Cooling**: 8-12°F below ambient (whole-house thermal envelope)
- **Reflectance**: 75-85% solar
- **Framework connection**: Photonic substrate response at the building envelope scale

### Level III: The Driveway
- **Application**: Lime-modified asphalt. Mix 5% hydrated lime into asphalt binder before aggregate. Compact and roll.
- **Cost**: ~$2/sqft
- **Cooling**: 15-25°F below standard asphalt (162°F → 137°F in Phoenix July sun)
- **Reflectance**: 35-45% solar (vs 5-15% for standard asphalt)
- **Emissivity**: 0.90+ in 8-13 µm
- **Framework connection**: Photonic substrate response at the ground surface scale

### Level IV: The Roof
- **Application**: Lime-modified roofing. White lime topcoat over standard roofing underlayment. Or: lime-modified cool roof coating (acrylic + lime + TiO₂)
- **Cost**: ~$3/sqft
- **Cooling**: 20-30°F below standard dark roof (180°F → 150°F)
- **Reflectance**: 75-85% solar
- **Emissivity**: 0.90+ in 8-13 µm
- **Framework connection**: Photonic substrate response at the high-irradiance scale (roofs receive 2-3x wall irradiance)

### Level V: The Walkway
- **Application**: Lime-modified concrete. Mix 10% hydrated lime into concrete mix. Trowel finish. Optional: pigment for color
- **Cost**: ~$5/sqft
- **Cooling**: 10-15°F below standard concrete (140°F → 125°F)
- **Reflectance**: 50-60% solar
- **Emissivity**: 0.88+ in 8-13 µm
- **Framework connection**: Photonic substrate response at the pedestrian scale

### Level VI: The Garden
- **Application**: Lime-modified soil. Broadcast 0.5 lb/sqft hydrated lime. Till to 6 inches. Water in.
- **Cost**: ~$1/sqft
- **Cooling**: 3-5°F reduction in ambient air temperature (transpiration + reflectance)
