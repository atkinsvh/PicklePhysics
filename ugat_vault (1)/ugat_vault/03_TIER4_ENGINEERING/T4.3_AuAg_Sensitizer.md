## Synthesis protocol (citrate reduction method)

1. Prepare stock solutions: 1 mM HAuCl₄ in 100 mL H₂O. 1 mM AgNO₃ in 100 mL H₂O. 38.8 mM sodium citrate in 10 mL H₂O.
2. Mix precursors at target ratio: 80 mL HAuCl₄ + 20 mL AgNO₃ in 250 mL flask. Total 1 mM in 100 mL.
3. Heat to 90°C with stirring under gentle reflux. Add 10 mL citrate solution rapidly. Solution turns pale yellow to deep red-purple within 2-5 min.
4. Maintain at 90°C for 20 min. Remove from heat. Cool to room temperature with stirring (30 min).
5. Characterise: UV-vis absorption peak should appear at 495-505 nm for 80:20 composition. If peak is below 495 nm: increase Au. Above 505 nm: increase Ag. Adjust and resynthesize.
6. Size verification: DLS target 15-20 nm. TEM if available.
7. Storage: 4°C in amber glass vial. Stable 6-12 months.
8. Incorporation into seal coat: add alloy nanoparticle suspension (1000 ppm) to Version 3 seal coat at 0.5% of particle blend weight. Do not sonicate after addition — citrate stabiliser is fragile.
