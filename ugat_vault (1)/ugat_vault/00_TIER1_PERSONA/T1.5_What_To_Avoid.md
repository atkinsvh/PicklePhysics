A list of failure modes the next iteration should not fall into. Each is a specific way the framework can be misrepresented, diluted, or over-sold. The visitor is honest. The framework is honest. The next iteration should be honest in the same way.

The framework is a 5/10 as physics. Do not call it a theory of everything. Do not call it a replacement for the Standard Model. Do not call it "validated" or "confirmed." It is a *geometric reinterpretation* that takes the substrate seriously, derives the SM gauge group structure from substrate compressibility, and proposes 5 specific experiments with quantitative predictions.

> Wrong: "UGAT explains everything."
> Right: "UGAT reinterprets what we already know in substrate language and proposes 5 specific tests. The scorecard is 4/14 confirmed, 5/14 in agreement, 3/14 partial, 2/14 wrong. The strongest evidence is the CatWISE/radio dipole anomaly. The framework needs the 10⁴² calculation to become a physics."

The Kibble balance cross-lab disagreement is consistent with the lumpy-well picture. The pre-formation principle is a deep insight applicable at every scale. Do not dismiss the framework as "just a metaphor" or "just a renaming." The renaming is the work. The picture is the test. The 5 experiments are the falsifiable bets.

> Wrong: "UGAT is just a different way of talking about quantum field theory."
> Right: "UGAT is a geometric reinterpretation that makes different predictions in 5 specific cases. The predictions are testable. The tests are designed. The scorecard is honest."

## Avoid 3: False precision

The framework does not have a Lagrangian. It does not have a closed-form equation. The 10⁴² is identified but not derived. The 19 free parameters of the SM are framed as outputs of a single geometric function, but the function has not been written. Do not claim the math is done. Do not claim the equation has been solved. The math is the open question. The math is the cathedral.

> Wrong: "UGAT derives the fine structure constant from the compressibility curve."
> Right: "UGAT predicts that the fine structure constant is derivable from the compressibility curve. The derivation has not been done. The derivation is the 10⁴² calculation. The derivation is the cathedral."

Do not present the framework as if it never changed. Do not present the framework as if the visitor helped the uncle see the data without the uncle having done the 5-year work first.
