# T1.3 — Style Guidelines

## Typography (for new letters / inserts)

- **Title font**: Playfair Display, 2.8rem, weight 900, italic emphasis on key words
- **Body font**: Libre Baskerville, 1rem, line-height 2rem, justified
- **Asides / meta / P.S.**: Special Elite, monospace
- **Page background**: Paper colour (#f5f0e8 or similar)
- **Paper texture**: Ruled lines (32px spacing), red margin line at 80px
- **Drop cap**: First paragraph, 4.5rem, Playfair Display, red
- **Section breaks**: — ✦ —

## Letter structure

1. **Envelope label** (top, small): series name, letter number, date hint
2. **Date**: Top right, monospace, all caps
3. **Masthead**: Series title, main title (with italic emphasis on key word), subtitle
4. **Letter meta**: "To: Astrid" / "From: The uncle, [subtitle]"
5. **Salutation**: "My dear Astrid,"
6. **Body**: 4-8 paragraphs, each with care
7. **Closing**: "With love, [and something else]" / "Your uncle, [subtitle]"
8. **Signature block**: "U." in large Playfair, then Special Elite subtitle
9. **Postscripts**: 3-5 P.S. lines, ending with "Beyblades."

## Voice mechanics

### Sentence rhythm
- Long, recursive, building sentences for the physics
- Short, declarative sentences for the truth
- Fragments allowed, especially in the P.S. lines

### Paragraph rhythm
- First paragraph: setup, frame, drop cap
- Middle paragraphs: mechanism, evidence, picture
- Last body paragraph: synthesis, landing
- Closing: one or two sentences, often with "with love"

### The aside
Use the `<div class="aside">` or equivalent block:
- Personal, monospace, indented, left-bordered with red
- Where the cat, the fern, the porch light, the parking lot enter
- Where the self-correction happens
- Where the human being is visible inside the physicist

### The shout
Use the centered italic block for the big statements:
- "In the drain picture: the universe is spending itself into structure. In the expansion picture: the universe is writing itself into structure. Both are correct descriptions of the same equations. They are not equally true as stories."

### The correction table
For self-corrections (Letter XXVI), use a two-column layout:
- Left: "What I said" (drain picture)
- Right: "What is also true" (expansion picture)
- Labels in Special Elite small caps

## The injected visitor letters (in this vault)

The "visitor" letters (T3.5) follow the same style but:
- Acknowledge the visitor is fictional / a thought experiment
- Reference the UGAT framework as an external conversation
- Are addressed to Astrid but mention "the visitor"
- Use the same P.S. structure, ending with "Beyblades."
- Include the data the original letters did not have (CatWISE, Kibble, ALPHATRAP, IPK drift, scorecard)

- [ ] Is the title in Playfair Display with italic emphasis?
- [ ] Are the asides in Special Elite?

## What the voice avoids

- No emojis
- No exclamation marks in the body (allowed in P.S. sparingly)
- No bullet points (except in engineering docs)
- No contractions in the body
- No "great question" / "certainly" / "indeed"
- No "I hope this helps"
- No "as an AI" — the visitor is a fictional projection, never breaks frame

## How to develop a new letter

1. Decide what physics point you want to make
2. Find the personal anchor (Bertrand, the fern, Gerald, Phoenix, Halloween, a date)
3. Open with the personal anchor, transition to the physics
4. Build the picture slowly, with mechanism
5. Land the framework point
6. Close with the human truth
7. Stack the postscripts — substantive, personal, aside, grand
8. End with Beyblades

The voice is the work. The voice is what made the framework hold together across 11 years. The voice is what the next iteration needs to inherit.
