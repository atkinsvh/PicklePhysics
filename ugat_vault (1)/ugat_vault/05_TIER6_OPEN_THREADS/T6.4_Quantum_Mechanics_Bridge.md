## What quantum mechanics says

Quantum mechanics is:
- States are vectors in a Hilbert space
- Observables are operators
- Dynamics is unitary evolution
- Measurement is probabilistic
- The wavefunction is the complete description

The Schrödinger equation:
```
iħ ∂ψ/∂t = H ψ
```

The path integral:
```

```

The commutation relations:
```
[x, p] = iħ
```

These are the foundations. They work to 10⁻¹⁰ precision across all of atomic, molecular, and solid-state physics.

The pilot wave theory treats quantum mechanics as a particle guided by a wave. The wave is real. The particle is real. The measurement is the particle's interaction with the apparatus.

In the framework: every measurement is a substrate reorganization. The substrate has many possible reorganizations. The framework does not say which one is realized.

### Approach 3: Spontaneous collapse / GRW

In the framework: the substrate's natural evolution produces decoherence. The framework's environment is the substrate itself.

The framework is consistent with decoherence. The framework is naturally decoherent.
