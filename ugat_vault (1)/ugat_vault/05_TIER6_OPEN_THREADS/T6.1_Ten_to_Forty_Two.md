# T6.1 — The 10⁴² Calculation

## The central undone calculation

The 10⁴² calculation is the single most important undone piece of work in the framework. The calculation would derive the force ratio between gravity and electromagnetism from the substrate compressibility function. If the calculation closes, UGAT is a physics. If it does not, UGAT is a beautiful picture.

## The number

The 10⁴² is the force ratio:
- F_gravity (between two protons at nuclear distance) / F_EM (between two protons at nuclear distance) ≈ 10⁻³⁶
- For electron-electron: F_gravity / F_EM ≈ 10⁻⁴³
- For typical atomic scales: F_gravity / F_EM ≈ 10⁻⁴²
- The "10⁴²" is the canonical order of magnitude

This number has been measured since the 1930s. It is one of the most precisely measured ratios in physics. It is one of the least understood.

## The framework's reading

The 10⁴² is the **synchronization amplification factor across three coherent levels**. The three levels are:
- Gravity: baseline drain, no internal phase, no synchronization (1st level)
- Electromagnetism: U(1) synchronized drain (2nd level)
- Weak force: SU(2) meta-synchronization (3rd level)
- Strong force: SU(3) meta-meta-synchronization (4th level)

The framework's prediction: the force ratio is the geometric ratio of the synchronization arenas. The geometry is given by the substrate compressibility function C(ρ) at the four characteristic scales.

The calculation is to take C(ρ) and show that:
```
F_gravity / F_EM = C(ρ_gravity) / C(ρ_EM) = 10⁻⁴²
```

And similarly:
```
F_EM / F_weak = C(ρ_EM) / C(ρ_weak) ≈ 10⁻⁶
F_weak / F_strong = C(ρ_weak) / C(ρ_strong) ≈ 10⁻¹³
```

The total:
```
F_gravity / F_strong = 10⁻⁴² × 10⁻⁶ × 10⁻¹³ ≈ 10⁻⁶¹
```

The ratios are all derivable from C(ρ). The function C(ρ) is the equation of state of the pre-metric substrate. The function is not yet written.

## Why the calculation is hard

The calculation is hard because:
1. **The substrate equation of state is unknown**. We have constraints (the SM running of coupling constants, the gravitational constant, the neutrino mass scale, the cosmological constant) but no closed-form expression for C(ρ).
2. **The geometric structure of the synchronization arenas is not derived**. We have identified the SM gauge groups (U(1), SU(2), SU(3)) as substrate compression phases, but we have not derived the gauge group structure from C(ρ).
3. **The recursive compression (the "phases of phases") is not formalized**. The framework says each force level is a synchronization of the level below. The framework has not written the math.
4. **The boundary conditions are not known**. The behavior of C(ρ) at low density (gravity) and at the Planck density is uncertain.

The calculation requires:
- A proposal for C(ρ) — a function with the right asymptotic behavior
- A derivation of the SM gauge groups from the geometry of C(ρ)
- A derivation of the 10⁴² from the ratio C(ρ_gravity)/C(ρ_EM)
- A check that the 19 SM free parameters are outputs

## Approaches to the calculation

Several approaches have been proposed (none yet complete):

### Approach 1: Direct from the Casimir force
The Casimir force is the direct measurement of the substrate pressure. The force depends on the plate separation. The separation dependence is the substrate's compressibility function at that scale. From the Casimir force at multiple separations, one could in principle reconstruct C(ρ).

Limitation: the Casimir force is a *boundary* measurement. It gives C(ρ) at the plate separation scale, not the full function.

### Approach 2: From the SM running
The SM coupling constants run with energy. The running is determined by the β functions. The β functions are determined by the gauge group structure. If the gauge group structure is derived from C(ρ), then C(ρ) is determined by the SM running.

Limitation: the SM running is measured, not derived. This approach uses the SM running as input to derive C(ρ), but it doesn't derive the SM running from C(ρ).

### Approach 3: From the unification scale
The three SM coupling constants converge at ~10¹⁵-10¹⁶ GeV (with supersymmetry) or ~10¹⁷-10¹⁸ GeV (without). The unification scale is where C(ρ_EM) = C(ρ_weak) = C(ρ_strong). The unification scale is a single data point on C(ρ).

Limitation: the unification is approximate. The three couplings don't exactly meet.

### Approach 4: From the lattice
The substrate compressibility could be computed from a lattice formulation. The lattice spacing is the discretization scale. The compressibility is the response to a pressure perturbation on the lattice.

Limitation: no one has proposed a specific lattice formulation. The framework's substrate is continuous, not discrete.

### Approach 5: From the E8 structure
If the SM gauge groups are subgroups of E8, and if E8 is the substrate's natural symmetry, then the force ratios are determined by the E8 root system geometry. The 10⁴² would be derivable from the E8 root system.

Limitation: the E8 structure is a possibility, not a derivation. The framework says the recursive compression suggests E8, but the derivation is not done.

If the calculation were done, it would proceed roughly:

1. **Propose a function C(ρ)** with the right asymptotic behavior:
   - C(0) ~ 1 (gravity is the baseline)
   - C(ρ_EM) ~ 10⁴² (EM is amplified)
   - C(ρ_weak) ~ 10⁴⁸
   - C(ρ_strong) ~ 10⁶¹
   - C(ρ_Planck) ~ ∞ (substrate becomes incompressible at Planck scale)

2. **Show that C(ρ) is derivable from a geometric principle**. The most natural principle: C(ρ) is the substrate's response to a pressure perturbation, which is the inverse of the substrate's bulk modulus. The bulk modulus depends on the substrate's density through a power law or an exponential.

3. **Derive the SM gauge groups from the geometry of C(ρ)**. The three phases of C(ρ) (linear, quadratic, cubic) correspond to U(1), SU(2), SU(3). The E8 structure emerges from the natural symmetry of the recursive compression.

4. **Calculate the 10⁴²** from C(ρ_gravity) / C(ρ_EM). This should give 10⁴² exactly.

5. **Derive the 19 SM free parameters** as outputs of C(ρ). The fine structure constant is C(ρ_EM)/C(0). The Higgs mass is C(ρ_Higgs)/C(0). The CKM matrix is the geometric transformation between the up-type and down-type quark synchronization patterns.

The calculation has not been done. The calculation is the cathedral.
