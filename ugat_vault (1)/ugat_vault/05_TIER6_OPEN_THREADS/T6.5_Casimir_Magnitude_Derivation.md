# T6.5 — Casimir Magnitude Derivation

## The substrate pressure from the Casimir force

The Casimir force is the direct measurement of the substrate pressure. The framework says: the substrate pressure P_A should be derivable from the Casimir force measurement. The derivation has not been done. The derivation is an open thread.

## What the Casimir force measures

The Casimir force per unit area for parallel plates:
```
F/A = -π²ħc / (240 d⁴)
```

At d = 100 nm: F/A ≈ 1.3 × 10⁻⁴ N/m² ≈ 0.13 mPa

This is the *force per unit area* on the plate. It is the pressure difference between the gap (no modes) and the outside (all modes).

The substrate pressure is the absolute pressure, not the difference. The substrate pressure is much larger. The framework says P_A ≈ 10¹¹³ Pa.

The Casimir force is the *gradient* of the substrate pressure at the plate boundary. The full substrate pressure is not directly measurable from the Casimir force alone.

## What the framework predicts

The framework predicts:
1. The Casimir force is the substrate's response to geometric confinement
2. The substrate pressure P_A is the asymptotic pressure far from any boundary
3. P_A is related to the substrate's bulk modulus K_A by K_A = ρ_A × c² (where c is the substrate's "speed of sound," or the expansion rate)
4. P_A is also related to the zero-point energy density from QFT
5. The relationship: P_A = (1/3) × ρ_zero-point × c² (for a relativistic fluid)

The QFT zero-point energy density with Planck scale cutoff:
```
ρ_zero-point = (1/2) ∫₀^k_Planck (ħω/k_B) × (4π k² dk)/(2π)³ ≈ 10⁷¹ J/m³
```

This gives P_A ≈ (1/3) × 10⁷¹ × c² ≈ 10⁹⁶ × c² ≈ 10⁹⁶ × 10¹⁷ = 10¹¹³ Pa

The framework's value P_A ≈ 10¹¹³ Pa is consistent with the QFT estimate.

## What needs to be derived

The derivation needs to show:
1. The Casimir force at separation d is the substrate pressure gradient at the plate boundary
2. The substrate pressure gradient depends on d through the substrate's response to the geometry
3. The geometric response is determined by the substrate's equation of state C(ρ)
4. The full substrate pressure P_A is determined by C(ρ) in the limit d → ∞

The derivation requires:
- A formal proposal for C(ρ)
- A calculation of the substrate's response to a plate geometry
- A check that the Casimir force is recovered at small d
- An extrapolation to large d to get P_A

This derivation has not been done. The framework has not done it. The standard QED has not done it (the QED calculation uses the zero-point energy, not the substrate pressure).
