- Cost: $5,000-$15,000
- Time: 1 year
- Goal: Test whether the substrate pressure can be made directional by asymmetric geometry
- Decisiveness: High
- Publishability: High

### Year 3-4: The condensed matter test
**Invention IV: Geometric Superconductivity Trigger** (T4.5)

- Publishability: High

### Year 4-5: The applied engineering test
**Invention I: Topology-Optimised Lime Seal Coat** (T4.2)

- Publishability: Medium (engineering journal)
- Why fifth: Most applied, lowest scientific stakes, but immediate practical value
