- H₀ = 73.50 ± 0.81 km/s/Mpc
- Method: Cepheid-calibrated Type Ia supernovae, distance ladder
- Reference: Riess et al. 2022 (arXiv:2112.04510)

- H₀ = 67.24 ± 0.35 km/s/Mpc
- Method: ΛCDM fit to Planck CMB power spectrum
- Reference: Planck Collaboration VI 2020 (A&A 641, A6)

### The discrepancy
- ΔH₀ = 6.26 km/s/Mpc
- σ = √(0.81² + 0.35²) = 0.88
- Significance: 7.1σ

- The discrepancy is robust
