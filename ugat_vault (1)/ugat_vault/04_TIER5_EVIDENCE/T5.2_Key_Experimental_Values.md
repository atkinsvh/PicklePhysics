## Cosmology

### The CatWISE dipole
- CatWISE2020 catalog (unWISE + WISE): 1.3 million galaxies
- Dipole amplitude: 2.7× the CMB kinematic prediction
- Significance: 5.7σ
- Direction: not identical to CMB dipole direction
- Reference: Secrest et al. 2021, 2022 (arXiv:2105.06540, 2206.12440)

### The radio dipole (NVSS + RACS + LoTSS)
- Combined radio galaxy sample: ~1.7 million sources
- Dipole amplitude: 3.67 ± 0.49 × the CMB kinematic prediction
- Significance: 5.4σ
- Direction: similar to CatWISE, different from CMB
- Reference: Ding et al. 2023 (arXiv:2304.05765)

### The Hubble tension
- Local distance ladder (SH0ES): H₀ = 73.50 ± 0.81 km/s/Mpc
- CMB (Planck 2018): H₀ = 67.24 ± 0.35 km/s/Mpc
- Discrepancy: 7.1σ
- Reference: Riess et al. 2022 (arXiv:2112.04510), Planck Collaboration VI 2020

### The cosmic microwave background
- Temperature: 2.7255 K (Mather et al. 1999, FIRAS)
- Anisotropies: ~10⁻⁵ (Planck 2018)
- Kinematic dipole: 3.362 ± 0.001 mK amplitude, direction (l, b) = (264.0°, 48.3°)

## Substrate physics (Casimir)

### Casimir force precision tests
- Lamoreaux 1997: first precision measurement, ~5% uncertainty
- Mohideen 1998: sphere-plate, 1% uncertainty
- Decca 2003: parallel plate, 0.1% uncertainty
- Krause et al. 2007: 0.1% at 0.5-3 µm separation
- Bimonte et al. 2012-2020: thermal corrections, 0.5-1% agreement
- All measurements agree with QFT prediction across 5+ orders of magnitude

### Casimir force magnitude (parallel plates)
- F/A = -π²ħc / (240 d⁴)
- At d = 100 nm: F/A ≈ 1.3 × 10⁻⁴ N/m² ≈ 0.13 mPa
- At d = 10 nm: F/A ≈ 130 Pa
