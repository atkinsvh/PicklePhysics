## What this means

### Radioactive decay

The half-life of a radioactive isotope is the time for half the atoms to decay. The framework says the half-life measures the *organization* required to reach the transition state, not the energy difference between initial and final states.

Example: Hafnium-178m2 has 2.45 MeV of available energy (more than enough to decay) and a 31-year half-life. The energy is available. The geometry is not. The Hf-178m2 nucleus needs to organize into the alpha-particle pre-formation geometry before the alpha can tunnel out. The 31-year half-life is the pre-formation time. The 2.45 MeV is the energy released when the pre-formation completes.

This is consistent with the "cluster model" of alpha decay (Greiner 1969) and the modern "pre-formation factor" calculations. The framework generalizes: *all* half-lives measure pre-formation, not energy.

### Enzyme catalysis

Enzymes are biological catalysts. The framework says enzymes provide the *transition state geometry* — they organize the substrate into the transition state configuration, lowering the activation energy by providing the geometry.

The activation energy is not lowered by the enzyme. The activation energy is *bypassed* by the enzyme's geometry. The enzyme does the pre-formation. The reaction rate is increased by orders of magnitude because the pre-formation time is shortened from seconds (or years) to microseconds.

The framework generalizes: *all* catalysts work by providing the transition state geometry. The pre-formation is the bottleneck. The catalyst is the geometry.

### Alpha pre-formation in nuclei

The alpha particle (helium-4 nucleus) is a particularly stable substrate vortex. In heavy nuclei, the alpha can pre-form inside the nucleus before tunneling out. The pre-formation probability determines the half-life.

The pre-formation probability depends on:
- The number of nucleons available (more nucleons → higher probability)
- The pairing energy (paired nucleons are more available)
- The shell structure (magic numbers are less available)
- The angular momentum of the emitted alpha

The Geiger-Nuttall law (log t₁/₂ ∝ Z/√E) is the empirical relation. The framework says the pre-formation probability follows this law because the alpha needs to organize into the alpha-4 geometry, and the geometry depends on the available nucleons and the tunneling energy.

The Anfinsen dogma (sequence determines structure) is the substrate principle: the amino acid sequence pre-determines the pre-formation pathway. The pathway is the funnel. The folded structure is the geometry.
