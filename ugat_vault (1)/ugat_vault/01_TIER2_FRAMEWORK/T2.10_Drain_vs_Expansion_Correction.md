Both descriptions are locally equivalent. The global picture is different.

Time moves forward because the pre-metric substrate is continuously unfolding into the spacetime dimension. The future is toward more of the substrate having been expressed as geometry. Time is not the direction of depletion. Time is the direction of articulation. The universe is not running down. It is still being written. Every moment, more of the pre-metric potential is expressed as metric reality. The present moment is the expansion front. We are on it. We are it.
