# Concentration and Harvest

> *From 40-gallon ferment to 6oz concentrated jar delivering therapeutic dose in one teaspoon daily.*

## The Concentration Target

**Current formulation:** 1oz (28g) three times daily = 84g/day
**Target format:** 1 teaspoon (5ml) daily from 6oz jar = one jar per month

**Required concentration:** 3–4× current formulation

Achieved through two compounding methods:
1. **70/30 botanical:brine ratio** (vs. standard 60:40) → 1.5× botanical density
2. **Freeze concentration of brine to 35% original volume** → 2.8× brine concentration
3. **Combined effect:** ~3–4× total

## Freeze Concentration (Primary Method)

No heat, no live culture kill, no thermal degradation:

1. Draw brine from mid-level of vessel (not top or bottom) — 25–30 liters per harvest cycle
2. Pour into shallow stainless trays
3. Freeze solid at −20°C (standard freezer)
4. Allow to begin thawing — drain the concentrated liquid as the first fraction thaws
5. Water (ice) crystallizes first, concentrating all dissolved compounds in the remaining liquid
6. Discard ice fraction (essentially pure water, compound-depleted)
7. Collect concentrated brine: 3–5× original compound concentration, live culture preserved

**Why the first fraction is most concentrated:** Ice nucleates from pure water preferentially. The last liquid to freeze contains the most solutes. The first liquid to thaw (as temperature rises back above 0°C) is the most concentrated fraction.

## Botanical Matter Harvest

Open mesh bags. Extract minced botanical matter into collection vessel. This is NOT spent material:

**What remains in the botanical matter after 120-day aqueous extraction:**
- All highly lipophilic compounds (AKBA logP 6–8, carnosic acid logP 4–5, thymol logP 3.3) — essentially no aqueous extraction
- Intact fiber matrix with prebiotic function undiminished
- Fermentation-enhanced compounds (biotransformed to more bioavailable forms)
- Phytic acid reduced (mineral bioavailability improved)
- Piperine concentrated in softened peppercorns (consume peppercorn = consume piperine)
- Thymoquinone in softer, more accessible nigella seed matrix

**The relish principle:** Brine + botanical matter together are superior to either alone. They are complementary delivery mechanisms for different compound polarity classes.

## Product Assembly

1. Combine concentrated brine with botanical matter at 40% brine / 60% botanical ratio
2. Fill 6oz mason jars
3. Use one-way valve lids for live product (CO₂ exit without O₂ entry)
4. Quality check: pH 3.0–3.5 in concentrated brine, complex sour-herbal aroma, characteristic trigeminal profile present
5. Label: batch number, harvest date, pH, botanical pack version

## Yield Calculation (40-gallon batch)

- Brine volume: 91L → concentrated to ~25L (×3.5 concentration)
- Botanical matter: ~55kg
- Product per jar: ~177ml (6oz), 60% botanical / 40% brine
- **Approximate yield: 200–225 jars per batch**
- At staggered 4-tank operation: ~200 jars monthly

## Serving Protocol

**Dose:** 1 teaspoon (5ml) daily at 3–4× concentration
**Timing:** With a fat-containing meal — dietary fat solubilizes lipophilic compounds from botanical matter during digestion (the lecithin system in the brine assists, but meal-fat absorption through the gut mucosa is the primary lipophilic absorption route)
**Introduction:** Start ½ teaspoon daily. Increase gradually. Use DAO enzyme supplement for first 4–6 weeks if histamine-sensitive.
