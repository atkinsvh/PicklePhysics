# Economics

> *The batch economics of four staggered 40-gallon fermentation tanks achieving monthly production from month 5 of operation.*

## Per-Batch Economics (Single 40-gallon tank)

### Input Costs

| Category | Estimated cost |
|---|---|
| Vegetable fraction (45kg) | ~$180 |
| Botanical pack (15kg) | ~$280 |
| Brine additives (glycine, ascorbic, P5P, glucarate, molasses, lecithin, black seed oil, glycerol) | ~$95 |
| Culture additions (honey rotation) | ~$45 |
| Salt (non-iodized, bulk) | ~$12 |
| Energy (temperature management, refrigeration) | ~$35 |
| Packaging (200 jars, lids, labels) | ~$180 |
| Consumables (mesh bags, strainers, testing supplies) | ~$40 |
| Water (filtered/dechlorinated) | ~$15 |
| Labor (estimated 20–25 hours per batch cycle) | ~$370 (at $15–18/hr) |
| **Total batch cost** | **~$1,250** |

### Output and Revenue

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Approximate jar yield | ~200–225 jars per batch |
| Working yield (accounting for QC failures, sampling, waste) | ~200 jars |
| Retail price target | $32–38 per 6oz jar |
| Gross revenue per batch | $6,400–$7,600 |
| Gross margin | ~81–83% |

### Four-Tank Staggered System

Four tanks staggered by 30 days = monthly production of one batch starting from month 5 of operation:

| Month | Activity |
|---|---|
| Month 1 | Tank 1 starts |
| Month 2 | Tank 2 starts |
| Month 3 | Tank 3 starts |
| Month 4 | Tank 4 starts |
| **Month 5** | **Tank 1 harvested → first production batch** |
| Month 6 | Tank 2 harvested → second batch |
| Ongoing | Monthly production cycle |

**Annual gross revenue at steady state (month 5+):**
- 12 batches × ~$7,000 average = **~$84,000 annual gross**
- Less batch costs: 12 × $1,250 = $15,000
- **Annual gross profit: ~$69,000**

### Scaling Considerations

The batch economics are favorable to scale because:
- Most fixed costs (equipment, setup) are absorbed in first year
- Labor efficiency improves with experience and routine
- Bulk purchasing reduces per-batch ingredient costs
- Customer retention is high (monthly subscription product)

Two operators working part-time (40 hours/month combined) can manage a four-tank operation with good systems.

## Pricing Strategy

**$35/jar (midpoint of $32–38 range):**
- Premium fermented food positioning (comparable to high-end kimchi/kombucha at $15–20/16oz)
- The 6oz concentrated format delivers equivalent compound dose to a much larger conventional ferment
- Monthly subscription ($35/month) is psychologically positioned as a food/supplement hybrid, not a pharmaceutical

**Price anchoring:**
- Quality supplements for the conditions targeted often run $100–300/month
- The product at $35/month is dramatically less expensive than the supplement stack it partially replaces
- At the same time, it's a premium food product with a compelling story

## The Seasonal Botanicals Premium

Rotating botanical packs with seasonal and specialty ingredients (hawthorn berries at peak, locally harvested centella, etc.) creates a foundation for limited-release or premium-tier products:
- "Spring harvest batch" with fresh centella, fresh herbs
- "Autumn botanical batch" with hawthorn, elder, rosehip additions
- Premium pricing on these batches: $45–55 per jar
