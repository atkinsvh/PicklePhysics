# Product Concept

> *A 120-day lactofermented botanical medicinal preparation sold as a traditional fermented food. The product exists at the intersection of ancient empirical practice and contemporary mechanistic understanding.*

## Product Description

**Format:** 6oz concentrated jar
**Dose:** 1 teaspoon (5ml) daily
**Duration:** One jar per month
**Concentration:** 3–4× compared to standard serving of the fermented preparation

## What It Is

A complex lactofermented botanical preparation combining:
- ~55–60kg of medicinal vegetables and botanical herbs per batch
- 91L of an emulsified medicinal brine formulated to carry both hydrophilic and lipophilic compounds
- 120 days of active lactofermentation by a community including Lactobacillus kunkeei and related honey-adapted organisms plus standard Lactobacillus species
- Freeze-concentrated to 3–4× to achieve therapeutic compound density at 1 teaspoon daily dose

## Regulatory Positioning — Critical

**Sell as:** Traditional fermented preparation / fermented food product

**Why this matters:** Health claims trigger FDA drug or dietary supplement regulations. A food product with no health claims:
- Is regulated under GRAS (Generally Recognized as Safe) and food GMP standards
- Requires no clinical trials for sale
- Can be sold at the price point the market will bear
- Is not subject to structure/function claim restrictions

**What can be said:** "120-day small-batch lactofermented botanical preparation." "Traditional fermented preparation." "Made with [list of botanicals]." The story and the ingredients speak for themselves to an informed customer.

**What cannot be said:** Disease claims, structure-function claims tied to specific conditions, "treats," "cures," "prevents."

## The Story Differentiator

The product story is:
1. A convergence of ancient wisdom (cross-cultural fermented medicinal preparations across Ayurveda, Islamic medicine, Chinese medicine, Mediterranean traditions)
2. A mechanistic explanation (what the compounds actually do, why fermentation improves bioavailability, why the brine emulsion system distributes compounds across polarity classes)
3. A production process that reflects the story (genuinely 120 days, genuinely small batch, genuinely 55kg of botanical matter per batch)

The product is not a supplement pretending to be food. It is food with a mechanistic framework explaining why it works. The distinction matters to the customer segment that will pay the price point.

## Serving Protocol for Customers

**Start:** 1/4 teaspoon daily for first two weeks
**Build:** Add 1/4 teaspoon per week until at 1 teaspoon daily
**Maintain:** 1 teaspoon daily with food

For histamine-sensitive customers:
- Start with 1/8 teaspoon
- Take with a DAO enzyme supplement for first 4–6 weeks
- Increase very gradually

## The Sensory Experience

A customer receiving a jar should notice:
- Deep purple-amber color in the brine
- Complex layered aroma: sour, herbal, earthy, mildly pungent
- Taste: pleasantly sour, complex bitter layer (quercetin, thymoquinone, other active compounds), savory/umami depth, mild salt
- Trigeminal: garlic, ginger, and slight pepper/cinnamon warmth
- Texture: crunchy vegetable matter in a slightly viscous, richly colored brine

The sensory experience should feel medicinal-but-delicious. The bitterness and complexity are features, not defects. An informed customer will understand that the bitterness = compound presence.
