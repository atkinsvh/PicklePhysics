## Non-Supplement Foundations
- **Resistance exercise:** collagen synthesis signal in tendons/ligaments — non-negotiable
- **Intermittent fasting 16:8:** mTOR-dependent autophagy
- **Sauna (if accessible):** HSP70/90; protein quality control
- **Cold immersion:** adipose lipophagy; beta-3 adrenergic pathway
- **Natto 2 tbsp daily:** K2 (MK-7); spermidine; nattokinase
- **Fresh broccoli sprouts:** sulforaphane — can't substitute with supplement equivalently
- **Bone broth:** glycine, hydroxyproline, collagen peptides in food matrix
