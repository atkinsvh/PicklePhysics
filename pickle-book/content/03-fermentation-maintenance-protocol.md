# Fermentation Maintenance Protocol

> *The full maintenance cycle for the 40-gallon medicinal ferment. Frequency is critical — the ferment is a living system that responds to management.*

## Daily
- Check brine level — top up with fresh 3.5% salt solution if evaporation has reduced level
- Visual check — no kahm yeast film on brine surface, all botanicals fully submerged
- Temperature check — record vessel temperature, confirm 18–22°C
- Sensory check — clean sour smell confirms healthy fermentation

## Weekly
- **pH measurement** — test brine with pH strips or meter, target 3.2–3.8 mature. Take corrective action if out of range.
- **Rotate one botanical bag group** — B1→B2→B3→B4 in staggered 4-week cycle. Never replace all groups simultaneously.
- **Add honey culture** — 200–300ml freshly activated honey culture from designated perpetual mother (rotating monthly variety)
- **Taste sample** — assess sourness, flavor complexity, off-notes

## Monthly
- Replace 25–30% of vegetable fraction with fresh minced material
- Add fresh glycine and ascorbic acid to brine (~20% monthly depletion)
- Full vessel interior inspection — check for deposits, discoloration
- Assess bubbling activity — mature ferment: slow steady bubbling is normal
- **Harvest** production quantity for concentration/packaging cycle
- Feed all perpetual mother culture jars (remove 50%, replace)
- Prepare new oxymel for the month's featured honey variety

## Quarterly
- Partial brine renewal — remove 20%, replace with fresh prepared brine
- 50% botanical pack replacement — all bag groups refreshed in staggered rotation
- Full emulsifier/carrier refresh (lecithin, black seed oil, glycerol)
- Production batch concentration cycle (freeze concentration of brine fraction)
- Viability check on selected glycerol culture stocks

## Annual
- **Reserve 10–15% brine as starter culture** — this established Lactobacillus community is the most valuable asset; label and refrigerate separately before emptying
- Full vessel empty and deep clean with hot water + food-grade vinegar rinse
- Air dry completely before restart
- Restart with fresh batch, 10% reserved brine as starter inoculum
- Comprehensive culture bank audit

## Contamination Identification

| Observation | Likely cause | Action |
|---|---|---|
| White film on surface | Kahm yeast (harmless) | Remove film, ensure submersion, lower temperature |
| Pink/red discoloration | May be normal (red cabbage) or contamination | Taste/smell assessment — if off-odor, partial brine renewal |
| Putrid/strongly rotten smell | Pathogenic contamination | Do not consume; investigate; possible batch restart |
| Strongly alcoholic | Yeast overgrowth | Lower temperature, check salt level |
| pH > 4.5 after 2 weeks | Fermentation not establishing | Check temperature, salt, water source (chlorine?); add mature brine |
| Slimy texture | Some Lactobacillus exopolysaccharide production | Usually safe; investigate if accompanied by off-odor |
